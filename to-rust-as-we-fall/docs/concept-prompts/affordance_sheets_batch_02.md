## Final prompt set

### Crust

Design a wall-bound colony, never a mobile creature. Use one irregular thick
waxy mat fused into broken institutional masonry. Its principal graphic is a
field of 20–30 large close-packed hexagonal crater-pores. The old cream center
is thick; the rust-tinted growing edge is thin and irregular; a torn edge shows
three or four stacked wax layers entering the wall. In the active state one
contiguous pore band dilates and projects a pale acid-vapor curtain. In the
damaged state a burned region flakes away to bare wall. Avoid a rectangular
machine panel, grille, beehive, face, limbs, and tiny high-frequency pores.

### Candid

Design one low floor-bound fungal territory with three unmistakable horizontal
bands: large fused yeast-cell lumps at the substrate, vertical chains of
pseudohyphal beads in the middle, and a sparse horizontal roof made from a few
alpha-cut filament cards. Surround the living colony with a much wider bleached
pH-shifted floor halo; that discoloration is the passive damage warning. The
burned state loses the canopy and most chains but retains a dark dormant basal
carpet. Avoid mushroom caps, a central creature, loose random scatter, and a
generic glowing poison puddle.

### Gnawer

Design one low, long, medium-dog-sized ratlike pack quadruped with an oily
near-black heme sheen. Use a narrow torso, angular shoulders, short wide-set
legs, a low wedge skull, and an oversized broad lower jaw hanging below the
chest line. The silhouette must work without haze. During wind-up it plants all
four feet, drops the jaw, and pushes a dense enzyme-mist wedge forward. The
dead body sprawls sideways while a low cloud lingers over the head. Avoid a
generic wolf, long rat tail, exposed tooth rows, strand fur, and armor.

### Capbage

Design one closet-scale tended plant from 10–14 large rigged curved leaf-shell
meshes in four concentric layers. In the open state those leaves frame one
large dark cavity with pale inner walls and a small meristem deep inside; the
negative space must read as shelter, never a mouth. In the sealed state the
same leaves fold into a tense near-sphere with broad overlap seams and faint
internal light. In death they split and wilt outward. Each leaf uses only a few
large facets and a strong cream rib. Avoid foliage cards, hundreds of leaves,
teeth, spider-leg shapes, and a smooth featureless sphere.
