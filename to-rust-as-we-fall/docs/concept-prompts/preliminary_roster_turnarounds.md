Generated with the built-in image generator on 2026-08-11. These sheets are

## Shared turnaround grammar

Every sheet presents one unchanged neutral asset at a consistent scale in this
left-to-right order:

1. true front orthographic;
2. true left-side orthographic;
3. front three-quarter;
4. true back orthographic.

Only the asset or camera rotates. Pose, anatomy, module footprint, authored
asymmetry, card assignment, and material state remain fixed. Environmental
organisms retain the minimum substrate or wall module needed to explain their
attachment. Sheets avoid labels, arrows, action effects, damage states, and
silhouette insets.

## Prompt pattern

Use the approved affordance sheet as a locked design reference. Render exactly
the same intact neutral subject four times at identical scale in front, left
side, front three-quarter, and back orthographic views. Rotate only the subject
or camera. Preserve the species-specific silhouette, topology, asymmetry,
attachment, card construction, and material grouping. Use a neutral charcoal
studio background, broad soft lighting, a faint contact shadow, and generous
padding. Do not add action effects, damage, alternate states, labels, arrows,
borders, signatures, or watermarks.

For Seefern and Hushbloom, the side view is also a construction proof: foliage
must collapse to the razor-thin edge of its assigned alpha-cut card. Painted
leaf silhouettes and transparent gaps belong to the card texture rather than
separate leaf meshes.
