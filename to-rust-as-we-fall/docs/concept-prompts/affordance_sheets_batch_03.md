## Final prompt set

### Tangler

Build the organism from two thick, irregularly coiled tau-like strands and a
small number of large hooked extensions. Keep the crown asymmetric and
front-loaded so the hooks read at encounter distance. Show a compact resting
silhouette, an opened attack posture, and an unwound defeated state. Avoid a
balanced antler crown, thin branch noise, perfect bilateral symmetry, and a
generic tentacle ball.

### Flare

Use one translucent faceted envelope around a clearly visible four-lobed
nucleus. Divide the interior particles into three unmistakable granule classes
using scale, shape, and color—not random speckle. The wind-up should compress
the envelope and organize the granules; the post-burst state remains alive,
deflated, and visibly recovering. Avoid implying that the burst consumes or
kills the organism.

### Toxo

Construct one compact crescent cell whose forward pole is dominated by a
spiral conoid and a small cluster of rhoptry bulbs. The body crescent and polar
tooling must survive as the distant silhouette. Show a directional lunge and a
curled defeated state. Avoid legs, a vertebrate face, or an armored insect read.

### Scarpet

Treat Scarpet as a low terrain-scale carpet made from sparse clumps of
alpha-cut sprig cards over a pale substrate, never as a mound or character.
Use broad green and rust zones that remain legible from above. Show tended,
senescent, and locally burned variations without arrows or UI marks. Avoid
uniform noise, dense lawn grass, and a continuous solid mesh canopy.

### Gasafoetida

Use one tall faceted stalk with a broad crown holding five to seven woody cone
pods. A cut in the stalk exposes a large amber resin bead as the primary
interaction affordance. Build the basal umbellifer foliage from flat alpha-cut
cards carrying finely divided feathery leaflets; do not use eye-like markings.
Show opened pod construction and a charred crown with basal recovery. Avoid a
Seefern silhouette, flower petals, or a generic explosive barrel.

### Climbvine

Use a thick segmented rope mesh that establishes a continuous climb line over
a wall or steep slope. At widely spaced nodes, attach only a few small rootlet
cards. A severed end must expose a large fibrous cross-section. Show fresh and
dead/dry material states while keeping the route silhouette continuous. Avoid
thin decorative ivy, dense leaves, and ambiguous disconnected strands.

### Mother Flure

Design a chamber-scale clonal source, not a character or boss creature. Use a
large anchored central body with a few heavy radial supports and a curtain of
clearly related Flure offshoot forms. Show dormant, cascade, and bloom-network
reads. Avoid a face, throne, mechanical cylinder, combat telegraph, or death
state that invents unsupported gameplay.

### Forget-me-nots

Build small ambient flower clusters from crossed foliage cards and simple
faceted blue flower heads. Their role is quiet environmental punctuation near
shelter, so keep the silhouette low and the color accent restrained. Avoid
emission, pickup framing, interaction halos, and mesh-built individual leaves.

### Resolution Roots

Show pale roots forming an incomplete ring whose missing or disrupted segment
is visible from the play camera. Include a cutaway demonstrating that the
surface tips connect through thicker underground filaments. Keep this sheet
puzzle-specific and botanical; do not depict or invent the unresolved
Chelator. Avoid teeth, crystals, a summoned creature, or a perfectly complete
ornamental circle.
