# Structures — generation-vocabulary body prompts

The fifteen structures in `data/generation/content_palette.json` are
district-agnostic BODIES (the ArchetypePieceLibrary generation manifest): the
same noun re-emits per district palette. These prompts render them in the
Plumbing Power Project palette (the built district); regenerate with another
district's material language when that district's pass begins.

Shared block:

> **[STYLE]** = Voxel and low-poly base geometry with painterly atmospheric
> textures applied over it. Hand-painted brush detail visible on every surface.
> Materials of the Plumbing Power Project: rusted riveted iron, dark worn wood,
> hexagonal grating, wet surfaces catching cyan/red accents on near-black.
> Diorama-on-dark, single structure centered, three-quarter view, game-asset
> concept sheet framing. No readable text anywhere — signage and screens carry
> abstract dot-glyph patterns only.

---

## Shelter (`shelter`) — rest / checkpoint

> [STYLE] A small lean-to rest shelter built against industrial wall plate: a
> plank roof on iron posts over a raised sleeping board and a bedroll, with a
> soft warm interior glow spilling out — clearly safe, clearly rest. Humble,
> hand-built, watertight.

## Portal (`portal`) — shortcut / revisit (DORMANT)

Portal law: dormant portals are DARK — no lens, no colored glow.

> [STYLE] A dormant transit portal: a free-standing ring of dark machined iron
> segments on a squat mount pedestal, its inner channel empty and UNLIT — dead
> metal, a faint cold rim light only. It reads as a machine waiting for power,
> not a working portal.

## Terminal (`terminal`) — Aster / data

> [STYLE] A slim data terminal pedestal: a dark iron cabinet at chest height
> with a narrow screen strip glowing terminal-green, displaying abstract
> dot-glyph blocks. One thin cable conduit runs from its base. Institutional,
> old, still working.

## Barrier (`barrier`) — Endo / boundary / repair

> [STYLE] A hand-repaired boundary barrier: a post-and-rail fence section of
> dark iron posts and wooden rails, with one rail visibly newer than the rest —
> a fresh plank patch strapped on with wire — marking a maintained edge of safe
> ground.

## Membrane (`membrane`) — organic gate

> [STYLE] A round organic membrane stretched inside an iron ring frame like a
> drumhead: taut translucent tissue, deep teal, with a faint slow glow at its
> center and radial tension wrinkles. It reads as a soft door — something that
> yields to the right touch.

## Pipe (`pipe`) — channel

> [STYLE] A heavy industrial pipe segment on cradle feet: a fat riveted iron
> tube with flanged ends and a small cross-wheel valve on top, streaked with
> rust and drips, wet highlights along its crown.

## Junction (`junction`) — route node

> [STYLE] A pipe junction block: a squat iron manifold where three flanged pipe
> stubs meet at right angles, with a stubby valve wheel on top and stenciled
> abstract glyph dots on its face. Compact, load-bearing, the map's crossroads
> made physical.

## ClassGate (`class_gate`) — gated passage

> [STYLE] A narrow personnel gate of dark vertical iron bars in a heavy frame,
> with one amber indicator block glowing above the lintel — a checkpoint that
> reads as selective: some pass, some do not.

## ShortcutGate (`shortcut_gate`) — one-way route

> [STYLE] A small square hatch-gate in an iron frame wall panel: a wooden door
> sized for crawling, hinged to swing one way only, with a simple drop-bar
> latch on the near side. It reads as a shortcut you open from THIS side.

## HideSlot (`hide_slot`) — concealment niche

> [STYLE] A person-sized concealment niche in dark iron plate: a shallow
> alcove with a shadowed interior deep enough to swallow a standing figure,
> its edges worn bright by use. The darkness inside is the feature.

## ForageCache (`forage_cache`) — supplies

> [STYLE] An octagonal storage basin with a hinged lid ajar: dark iron sides, a
> soft teal shimmer of stored goods visible through the gap, a small hasp. It
> reads as a cache someone stocked and means to return to.

## CarryGear (`carry_gear`) — hauling equipment

> [STYLE] A wooden carry-frame rack: an A-frame of worn timber with hanging
> straps and a small strapped crate at its foot, ready to be shouldered.
> Human-scale logistics, no machinery.

## RootSlide (`root_slide`) — descent

> [STYLE] A polished wooden slide chute descending along a root-like curve: a
> plank trough worn smooth and slightly glossy from use, its lip rounded, with
> a small teal marker glow at the mouth. Reads as a fast one-way down.

## WaterControl (`water_control`) — flow control

> [STYLE] A floor-standing valve column: a dark octagonal iron pillar at waist
> height crowned by a four-spoke rust-brown control wheel, drip streaks down
> its flanks, a faint cyan sheen from nearby water light.

## Workbench (`workbench`) — crafting

> [STYLE] A maker's workbench against a dark pegboard panel: a sturdy wooden
> table, a small vice block, scattered hand tools on hooks, one small screen
> box with a green dot-glyph readout clamped to the panel edge. Working
> clutter, warm wood against dark iron.
