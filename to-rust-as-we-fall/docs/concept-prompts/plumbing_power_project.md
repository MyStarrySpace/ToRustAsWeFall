# Plumbing Power Project — prop concept prompts

District props for the wash relay stretch (asset-sheet CHANNELS PROPS section).
Each prompt is self-contained. The district style block baked into every prompt:

> **[STYLE]** = Voxel and low-poly base geometry with painterly atmospheric
> textures applied over it. Hand-painted brush detail visible on every surface.
> Setting: the Plumbing Power Project — a vast dark cistern-shaft of rusted
> riveted iron, wet wooden walkways, and hexagonal grating, where the only light
> is glowing cyan water, red bar lamps, purple portal energy, and blue-violet
> bioluminescence against near-black. Wet surfaces catch and reflect the colored
> light. Diorama-on-dark: single prop centered, isolated against the void,
> three-quarter hero view, game-asset concept sheet framing.

Below, `[STYLE]` stands for that block — paste it in place of the marker.

---

## VeinTrunk (`vein_trunk`) — plate 3 hero organic

Silhouette priority: linked bulbous segments — a gnarled chain of swollen
knuckles, never a smooth or conical trunk. Affordance: none (ambient overgrowth).

> [STYLE] A thick organic vein-trunk climbing a wall of riveted iron plate: a
> living vascular growth the color of dried blood, built of linked bulbous
> segments like swallowed knuckles, each bulge glossy and wet. Smaller side
> branches split off and curl across the iron. At the base the trunk splays into
> gripping root fingers over wooden planks, and a cluster of small violet and
> blue bioluminescent bulbs nests among the roots, casting colored light up the
> trunk. Height about two people tall.

## BiolumeCluster (`biolume_cluster`) — plates 1/3/4

Silhouette priority: squat glowing mushroom caps + one or two angular crystal
shards, on a dark rooted pad. This is the SOURCE of all blue/violet ambient glow.

> [STYLE] A small cluster of bioluminescent growths on a dark fibrous root pad:
> five or six squat mushrooms with softly glowing caps, some blue, some violet,
> on pale stems, crowded together with one or two angular translucent crystal
> shards growing among them at a tilt. The caps and shards are the light source,
> pooling blue-violet light onto the wet surface beneath. Knee-high at most.

## Porthole (`porthole`) — plate 3

Silhouette priority: octagonal riveted frame + cross-spoke wheel over a lit
teal window. Affordance: a look INTO the reservoir drum.

> [STYLE] An octagonal porthole assembly mounted proud of a curved riveted iron
> drum wall: a heavy rust-brown octagonal frame studded with rivet heads, a
> four-spoke wheel mullion crossing the opening, and behind the spokes a bright
> teal window of underwater light with small drifting bubbles. The window glow
> spills onto the drum plates around the frame.

## DeckPlanks (`deck_planks`) — plates 3/4

> [STYLE] A section of walkway decking from a wooden pier: seven weathered
> planks side by side with narrow gaps, alternating fresh and grey-worn boards,
> two sitting slightly proud from foot traffic, crossed near the ends by thin
> dark iron nail strips. The wood is damp — a wet sheen catches faint cyan light
> from one side and warm red from the other.

## DeckGrate (`deck_grate`) — plates 3/4

> [STYLE] A square industrial floor grate set into a riveted iron frame: a
> lattice of dark metal bars with open square holes, darkness underneath, and a
> faint cyan water-glow bleeding up through the mesh from below. Worn edges,
> chipped paint on the frame, wet highlights on the bar tops.

## WallTracery (`wall_tracery`) — plates 1/3

Silhouette priority: pointed-arch tracery FORMED BY the veins — the wall reads
as claimed by the organism, architecture and growth in one.

> [STYLE] A tall dark iron wall panel completely overgrown by organic
> vasculature: dried-blood vein tendrils climbing the plate and joining into two
> pointed gothic arches, a thicker knuckled trunk rib climbing between them,
> thin connector veins stitching rib to rib, and small nodule knobs along every
> tendril. At several junctions, tiny blue and violet bioluminescent bulbs bud
> from the veins and light the tracery. The iron shows through between the
> growth. About twice a person's height.

## DoorIronband (`door_ironband`) — plate 3

> [STYLE] A heavy workshop door in a dark iron frame: warm brown wooden planks
> strapped by three horizontal riveted iron bands, a small iron pull handle,
> the wood worn pale around it. Wet iron catches a faint red lamp glow from
> above; the recesses hold deep shadow.

## RedBarLamp (`red_bar_lamp`) — plates 1/4

> [STYLE] A horizontal industrial light bar mounted on a wall over a doorway: a
> glowing deep-red tube gripped by two square iron end brackets under a slightly
> wider dark rain hood. The red light pools down the wet wall and floor below
> it. Compact, about an arm-span wide. The only light source in frame.

## GateSign (`gate_sign`) — plate 4

Wordless-text law: the readout is an ABSTRACT dot pattern, never letters.

> [STYLE] A large rectangular signage panel hung over an industrial gate lintel:
> a dark iron frame around a deep-red perforated dot-matrix face, its dots lit
> in an abstract blocky glyph pattern — deliberately unreadable, no letters or
> numbers. A single thin cyan neon strip runs along the underside of the frame.
> Two dark mount tabs bolt it to the lintel above.

## PortalRingOrnate (`portal_ring_ornate`) — plate 2 hero

Portal law: purple energy; the ring is dark machined iron when dormant.

> [STYLE] A thick free-standing portal ring on an integral mechanical mount: a
> segmented torus of blocky machined iron greebles — plates, lugs, and stepped
> housings — with an inner ring of intense purple neon light. Through the open
> lens, a glimpse of a lush green garden with a hanging yellow lamp, impossibly
> bright against the dark industrial shaft around it. The ring stands on a
> radial-plank wooden pier; purple light pools on the wet boards.

## PortalConsole (`portal_console`) — plate 2

> [STYLE] A waist-high pedestal control console beside a portal: a dark iron
> body with a slightly tilted screen face glowing violet-purple, displaying
> abstract blocky glyphs — no readable text. Two ball-jointed cables run from
> its back down into the floor. The screen glow lights the underside of a
> nearby portal ring segment.

## PortalPadRings (`portal_pad_rings`) — plate 2

> [STYLE] A circular floor pad of concentric machined rings set flush into
> wooden planking: alternating bands of dark iron and inset grooves, one thin
> ring of purple neon light glowing between them, and a raised round iron boss
> at the center. Seen at a low angle so the ring relief and the purple glow
> line read clearly.

## BallJointPipe (`ball_joint_pipe`) — plate 2

> [STYLE] A run of industrial piping snaking low along a wall: square-section
> dark pipes stepping in a zigzag, joined at every bend by a swollen spherical
> ball joint of blue-grey steel with a faint machined seam. One short riser
> climbs the wall. The ball joints catch a purple-and-cyan sheen from nearby
> lights; the pipe bodies stay dark and rusty.

## WaterChannel (`water_channel`) — plates 1/4

Silhouette priority: the WATER is the light source; edges are organic, never a
neat rectangle.

> [STYLE] A glowing water channel cut through an industrial deck: brilliant cyan
> water running in an irregular, organically-edged trough sunk below hexagonal
> floor grating and plank edges, with small white foam flecks drifting on the
> surface. The cyan glow is the dominant light, up-lighting the grate bars and
> plank ends along both banks.

## ReservoirPlatform (`reservoir_platform`) — plate 1

> [STYLE] A small octagonal iron platform standing alone in dark teal water
> inside a huge reservoir drum: a squat riveted deck with a raised rim, a small
> dark access hatch on top, and a single feed pipe with one spherical ball
> joint running from the platform's edge away across the water. Lit coldly from
> above by a white-cyan ring light; the water around it is deep teal and still.

## BrokenPier (`broken_pier`) — plate 2

> [STYLE] The broken end of a wooden pier jutting over a black pit: five planks
> with staggered, splintered, jagged ends — no two the same length — one board
> sagging lower than the rest, short snapped stubs beyond the shorter planks,
> and a dark iron support beam under the intact end. The wet broken edges catch
> a thin red light from one side; below is void.
