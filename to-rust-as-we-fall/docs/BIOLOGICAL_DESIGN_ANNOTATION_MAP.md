# Biological Design Annotation Map

This document is the annotation authority for concept sheets. It records what
biological or chemical source is being translated, where that source appears in
the design, and whether the translation is anatomical, material, behavioral, or
transformational. It complements `CONCEPT_ART_STATUS_INDEX.md`; it does not grant
approval to preliminary artwork or reopen locked designs.

## Required annotation format

Every new silhouette, construction, state, or turnaround sheet must include:

1. **Exact source reference:** the actual molecule, organism, pathology, or plant
   image/diagram used, shown visibly on the sheet with its source name.
2. **Source extraction:** three to five large source features only. Do not map
   every atom, organelle, leaflet, or microscopic detail.
3. **Design mapping:** numbered callouts connecting each extracted feature to a
   visible silhouette mass, anatomical part, material cue, affordance, or state
   transition.
4. **Translation type:** mark each callout **A** (anatomy/silhouette), **M**
   (material/color), **B** (behavior/affordance), or **T** (transformation/state).
5. **Distance test:** identify which one or two mappings remain legible from the
   normal top-down gameplay camera.
6. **Drift audit:** label visually prominent features with no source, gameplay,
   construction, or world-language justification as **ORNAMENTAL DRIFT**. Remove
   or justify them before approval.

Biology is not always literal anatomy. A design may preserve its source through
deployment logic, state change, material behavior, or ecology. Do not invent a
false anatomical resemblance when the intended translation is behavioral.
