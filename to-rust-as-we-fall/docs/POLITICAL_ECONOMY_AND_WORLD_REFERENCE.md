# NV Unit Political Economy and World Reference

**Canonical seed / current working thesis:** the NV Unit organized its physical society and simulation to eliminate envy. Physical society was divided into maintenance, militaristic, and managerial classes with subclasses; managers were the primary users of Psyknapses and personalized simulation, while class separation and curated accounts of each function shaped comparison across the whole experiment. It succeeded: envy was genuinely eliminated rather than merely hidden or underreported. That success removed a noisy but necessary social feedback signal: the felt gap between present conditions and possible conditions. Desire was resolved privately or within class silos instead of becoming shared diagnosis, collective aspiration, or pressure on institutions. Residents remained genuinely content while their relationships, common world, and material support systems stagnated and decayed. In the physical world, vasculature brought natural resources into common circulation for distribution to everyone; physical money remained distinct from simulation-only facility credits.

**Purpose.** This is the single consolidation reference for the social thesis, political economy, philosophical architecture, material ecology, institutional history, and creature-origin logic of *To Rust As We Fall*. It gathers the governing canon from the GDD, later director clarifications, build-facing documents, and the project's ferroptosis/glia research origin, while labeling connective synthesis and unresolved decisions.

This does not replace encounter, character, or level specifications in the GDD. It is the governing reference when those documents use an older version of the world's economic or thematic premise. In particular, the director's 2026-07-29 clarification that facility credits are simulation-only supersedes the GDD's older section 5.8 account of credits as the civilization's general currency.

## 1. Canonical social thesis

### 1.1 The experiment and its genuine success

**ESTABLISHED — GDD + DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

The NV Unit was a social experiment intended to eliminate envy. It deliberately divided the physical society into maintenance, militaristic, and managerial classes with subclasses, while the managerial class became the primary user of Psyknapses and the simulation. The experiment worked in the narrow terms by which it understood the problem: envy was genuinely absent rather than displaced into a hidden but otherwise unchanged feeling. Residents were not merely forced to say they were happy. Their satisfaction was real.

Its mechanisms include:

- abundant personalized simulation used primarily by the managerial class;
- curated information and comparison;
- specialized roles formalized into maintenance, militaristic, and managerial class families with subclasses;
- physical separation among the three primary classes;
- state ownership of children, justified as protecting children's potential while relieving parents of responsibility they were handling badly;
- opposite-direction class narratives that make every class read the others as less desirable;
- wellness systems and satisfaction metrics;
- bounded simulation enhancements;
- facility credits used only for those enhancements;
- supervisor approval of spending;
- automation and living infrastructure that make the common material world easy to ignore.

The tragedy requires that this arrangement confer real goods. Security, pleasure, identity, mastery, relief from status anxiety, and protection from overwhelming comparison are not fake merely because the system built around them becomes lethal.

### 1.2 What envy means in this design

**ESTABLISHED CORE + SYNTHESIS**

Envy is not secretly a moral virtue, nor is every envious response accurate or constructive. It is a painful, noisy carrier of comparative information:

> **Another person's life can reveal a possibility, deprivation, or disparity in one's own.**

That information can produce resentment, cruelty, imitation, aspiration, solidarity, criticism, invention, or demands for change. A healthy society must regulate the destructive outputs without making the underlying comparison structurally illegible.

The NV Unit did not make that distinction. It treated the painful signal as the pathology and removed both the pain and the information it carried.

### 1.3 The lost feedback loop

**GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

The relevant contrast is not envy versus virtue. It is an open social feedback loop versus a closed satisfaction loop.

| Stage | Open social loop | NV Unit loop |
|---|---|---|
| **Perception** | A person encounters a life, need, achievement, or disparity outside their own position | Physical class separation, class narratives, and—for managerial users—curated simulation limit or pre-interpret comparison |
| **Error signal** | The gap between what exists and what could exist becomes emotionally legible | The gap is categorized as envy, maladjustment, or an unfulfilled private preference |
| **Expression** | People compare accounts, argue, imitate, organize, compete, cooperate, or invent | The want remains private or class-bounded; managerial wants can be resolved within curated experience, and an optional enhancement may require a credited request |
| **Decision** | Other people and shared institutions must respond | The common world need not respond; within managerial simulation, a supervisor authorizes, modifies, or denies credited enhancement requests |
| **Outcome** | The common world may change, for better or worse | The person's or class's experience changes while the common world remains untouched |
| **Institutional evidence** | Conflict and dissatisfaction reveal unresolved conditions | Low complaint and high satisfaction appear to prove the system is healthy |

The system therefore has abundant first-order feedback—clicks, preferences, reward responses, wellness scores—but loses second-order feedback about whether its categories, institutions, and shared direction should change.

### 1.4 Credits, enhancements, and supervisors

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

The facility has **credits**, a separate currency with value only inside the simulation environment. Credits can be spent on simulation enhancements, and spending requires supervisor approval.

Credits are therefore not:

- the physical civilization's general currency;
- a direct claim on iron;
- payment for biological necessities;
- the unit used by physical transit or access gates;
- autonomous purchasing power merely because a balance exists.

**SYNTHESIS**

They quantify a resident's ability to **request** changes to an experience. Supervisors retain the authority to decide which credited desires may become real inside the simulation. A credit balance and permission to use it are distinct.

This creates three layers of simulated desire:

1. **balance** — the credits the resident currently holds, with their source still open;
2. **catalog** — what the simulation makes imaginable and expressible as an enhancement;
3. **approval** — a supervisor's authorization, potentially framed through health, safety, fairness, or experimental compatibility; the actual criteria remain open.

The Aster simulation already shows the reward logic in miniature: the system attributes a maintenance success to Aster, refuses his attempt to recognize the support crew, and rewards him with a drink-machine upgrade. Peris's simulation is structured around wellness, overtime, sanctions, and care monitoring. The mature experiment can therefore reward people generously while controlling attribution and filtering what counts as an acceptable want.

The exact rules for earning, transferring, pooling, inheriting, or losing credits remain **OPEN**. Supervisor criteria and appeal rights also remain **OPEN**.

**Design invariant:** whatever those rules become, the approval system must plausibly preserve genuine contentment. It cannot behave like an ordinary scarcity shop that routinely leaves residents frustrated and envious. A denial may redirect, reframe, defer, or satisfy the underlying want another way; the exact mechanism remains open.

### 1.5 Disconnection and stagnation

**SYNTHESIS**

The NV Unit does not stagnate because contented people are lazy or because all innovation requires competition. It stagnates because consequential dissatisfaction is repeatedly routed away from the shared world.

- A desire that might have become a public claim remains private or bounded within one class; for managerial users, it may receive a curated resolution or optional enhancement.
- A comparison that might have connected two classes is prevented by physical separation or pre-interpreted so that each sees the other's life as undesirable.
- For managerial users, a novel simulated aspiration must fit the catalog and a supervisor's approval categories.
- A maintenance failure outside managerial users' curated experience cannot compete with immediately satisfying simulated feedback.
- Class silos reduce dependence on people in other functions for discovery, recognition, and collective projects.
- An institution that sees little complaint cannot distinguish genuine flourishing from successful insulation.

Residents may still create, care, play, and form attachments. What atrophies is the capacity to create a **shared diagnosis and shared future**. Society remains active at the level of individual experience while becoming historically closed.

> **Everyone had what they wanted, but no one could tell that the world they shared was dying.**

### 1.6 Simulation systems are related but not identical

Keep these layers distinct in writing:

| System | Function | Current status |
|---|---|---|
| **Baseline simulation / curated perception** | The clean, mediated environment through which the envy intervention operates; it is used primarily by the managerial class, while access and use among the other physical-world classes remain open | **ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION; FULL SCOPE OPEN** |
| **Facility credits** | Closed-loop currency for optional simulation enhancements | **ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION** |
| **Supervisor review** | Required authorization before credits can be spent | **ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION** |
| **Simulo-care** | A specialized simulation-based care service whose apparent opt-in access is actually metrics- and credential-gated | **ESTABLISHED — GDD** |
| **Psyknapse** | Interface and credential gate used primarily by the managerial class to access the simulation and related services | **ESTABLISHED — GDD + DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION** |
| **The Board** | Peris's wellness feed: representation and measurement of care substituting for direct relation | **ESTABLISHED — GDD** |

“Primarily” does not mean exclusively. The militaristic class is taught to avoid Psyknapses and simulation, while the maintenance class culturally dismisses them; whether and how members of either class can nevertheless gain access remains **OPEN**. Do not infer that exclusion from premium simulo-care necessarily means exclusion from the baseline simulation or from the society's broader satisfaction mechanism. The relationship between a supervisor who approves enhancements and the credential systems that gate specialized services also remains open.

### 1.7 Positional inequality is secondary, not the whole thesis

**SYNTHESIS**

Material and administrative inequality can persist beneath genuine contentment without producing consciously felt envy. Objective advantage appears through physical wealth, privileged access to Psyknapses and simulation, proximity to the facility, valid tags, schedule flexibility, housing stability, access to uncurated information, exemption from reassignment, supervisor trust, and recognition for invisible labor.

But concealed hierarchy is not the sole or even deepest failure. The experiment could remain dangerous even if its distribution were unusually fair. Its primary structural mistake is disconnecting subjective satisfaction from the condition of the shared material and social world.

The hierarchy matters because it compounds the feedback failure: people in different positions cannot assemble a common picture, and those with approval or administrative authority are most insulated from the conditions their decisions govern.

### 1.8 No singular villain

**ESTABLISHED CORE + SYNTHESIS**

- The founders can sincerely eliminate envy and reduce suffering.
- Residents' happiness can be genuine.
- Supervisors can believe their review prevents harm and positional competition.
- Standardization can provide real security.
- Functional specialization can provide mastery and identity.
- Care workers can do real good inside institutions that conscript care into discipline.
- Informal markets can restore comparison and voice while also enabling exploitation.

The catastrophe should emerge from ordinary people performing reasonable functions inside a system that has lost the feedback needed to revise those functions.

### 1.9 Naming

Use **NV Unit** for the civic experiment and the society organized around it.

Current technical documentation uses **NVU** for the neurovascular ecosystem, and the Aster simulation uses an `NVU-MAINT` task code. The civic and biological readings should remain deliberately adjacent. Whether “NV Unit” was always an explicit neurovascular reference, or whether characters understand the equivalence, remains **OPEN**.

## 2. Philosophical architecture

### 2.1 Arendt: ordinary function becoming catastrophe

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

Arendt supplies the game's register of responsibility and judgment:

- the monstrous structure need not have a monster behind it;
- institutional actors can produce catastrophe by competently performing bounded roles;
- procedural innocence does not erase responsibility;
- judgment becomes necessary where rules no longer describe the living situation;
- action must sometimes occur without certainty or institutional permission.

The canonical gameplay image is the neutral enforcer filing a closure order, cataloguing equipment, and doing the assigned job to death. The late-game *Life of the Mind* fragment and Aster's attempt to write into Arendt's unwritten volume on judgment externalize the positive question: what does it mean to choose and act when no completed theory can do the choosing for you?

Arendt supplies the institutional and judgment register. The more particular chain of responsibility, trust, secrecy, and transmission comes from Derrida below.

### 2.2 Foucault: power produces the people it measures

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

Foucault supplies the institutional machinery:

- scan grids, checkpoints, tags, and protocols make the population legible;
- disciplinary power operates continuously through classification and normalization, not only through spectacular punishment;
- the institution produces categories such as coherent, healthy, productive, eligible, and deviant, then treats those categories as natural facts;
- people internalize the categories and police one another without direct orders;
- a helpful apparatus can become coercive precisely because participation appears voluntary and beneficial.

Supervisor approval of simulation enhancements belongs here. It does not merely deny purchases. It shapes the field of expressible desire and teaches residents which wants count as healthy, fair, or intelligible.

### 2.3 Derrida: responsibility, association, secrecy, and transmission

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

Derrida supplies the positive relational spine:

`particular responsibility → earned trust → shared secrets → resistance to institutional capture → transmission`

Responsibility begins with a person or situation that cannot be reduced to a general rule. Sustained responsibility earns trust; trust permits knowledge to cross boundaries; shared knowledge resists the institution's ability to isolate and autocomplete each perspective; transmission allows correction to outlive any one knower.

Hauntology is the failed version of transmission. Dead researchers remain active only as decontextualized records, suppressed cure work persists in fragments, old tags still register after bodies are gone, and Peris can be institutionally erased while physically present. The institution is full of signals from the past but has lost the relationships required to interpret them.

The cure components externalize relational practices rather than substituting for them. The best ending works because responsibility and knowledge have crossed positions and generations, not because the party found a purely technical object.

#### 2.3.1 *Donner la mort* and the Ouroboros scene

**DIRECTOR CREATIVE PROVENANCE**

The beginning of Jacques Derrida's *Donner la mort*—published in English as *The Gift of Death*—was the direct creative germ for the Ouroboros scene. Record that lineage even though the scene is neither a dramatization of the book nor intended to summarize its argument.

The scene's finished meaning belongs to this game: a civilization has treated its history and interpretive system as complete, so every apparently new thing can only be incorporated into and recirculated through the material already given. Peris's disclosure that the world can change introduces the possibility that the loop is not total. The work supplied a point of departure; the game's closed-loop social and systems argument is its own transformation of that encounter.

#### 2.3.2 *Différance*, association, and Peris's therapeutic insight

**DIRECTOR CREATIVE PROVENANCE + GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

The relevant term is Derrida's *différance*—spelled with an **a**—rather than ordinary French *différence*. The director's generative reading is that meaning is not finally secured by one self-contained pairing of signifier and signified. It emerges and remains revisable through a potentially unending network of differences, traces, deferrals, contexts, and connections to other signs.

That framing changed how the director understood relationships between things and eventually generated a central insight for Peris: the power of therapy can lie in helping someone alter the associations through which a memory, sensation, word, person, identity, or event acquires its force.

Peris does not discover that therapy can delete the past, install a correct interpretation, or simply replace one fixed meaning with another. She discovers that a person can form additional connections around what happened. A cue that once led only to fear, shame, obedience, isolation, or repetition can be connected to safety, language, another person, a different account of the self, and a new possible action. The old relation remains part of the chain without remaining its only possible destination.

In this context, “more productive associations” means **more livable, adaptive, and generative associations**, not associations that make a resident more compliant, measurable, or economically useful. That distinction separates Peris's practice from the NV Unit's institutional model of care:

- the institution assigns a category and manages the person toward it;
- Peris attends to the particular person's existing web of relations;
- therapeutic change expands what the person can feel, mean, and do rather than making one authorized meaning compulsory;
- the therapeutic relationship can itself become one of the new connections through which earlier experience is recontextualized.

This is the project's interpretive model of Peris and therapy, not a claim that Derrida proposed a clinical method or that every therapeutic practice can be reduced to reassociation. It should guide Peris's dialogue and micro-content through changed repetitions, contexts, and relational possibilities rather than speeches that explain the philosophy.

### 2.4 Adjacent lenses

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

- **Simmel's blasé attitude:** overstimulation dulls the ability to register meaningful difference.
- **Huxley rather than Orwell:** the population is not primarily censored into submission; it is satisfied and overstimulated away from sustained public attention and collective correction.
- **Debord:** dashboards, wellness feeds, and simulated representation substitute for direct social relation.
- **Goodhart:** once satisfaction, wellness, throughput, inspection, or coherence becomes a target, the proxy displaces the condition it was meant to reveal.
- **Georg Blind's rule-based economics:** a living economy requires variation, selection, retention, and the capacity to change its rules, not merely optimize within them.
- **Polanyi:** disembedded systems provoke protective countermovements that can themselves become destructive.

These are lenses on the same causal structure, not competing explanations pasted onto separate districts.

### 2.5 Anamnesis as the positive program

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

The game does not resolve its politics by choosing growth over stability, markets over planning, or individual desire over collective provision. Both growth and stability become lethal when the system cannot feel when to risk and when to hold.

**Anamnesis** is restoration of error correction: attention to present conditions, recovery of suppressed memory, cross-perspective contact, care, transmission, and the capacity to re-choose rules and roles. It is not nostalgia or the retrieval of a perfect lost order. The best ending cannot merely find the missing answer because part of the answer was never written. Someone must judge and act.

The constructive claim is:

> **A society dies by losing the capacity to feel its own condition and heals by restoring that capacity.**

Care is how that capacity becomes material. Tending, maintenance, trust, rest, local knowledge, and responsibility reconnect action to consequence. They are not sentimental alternatives to systems; they are the work by which a living system remains corrigible.

### 2.6 Characters carry different missing perspectives

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

| Character | Political/systemic function |
|---|---|
| **Aster** | The correct error detector without operational authority. His arc moves from dashboards, prediction, and certainty toward embodied judgment and action. |
| **Peris** | Specific relational responsibility trapped inside a generalized professional role. She resists session and care-policy limits, yet discovers that the caseworker frame itself still kept her from fully seeing clients. |
| **Endo** | Invisible barrier labor and the worker perspective excluded from services calibrated around more legible classes. |
| **Myke** | Maintenance labor, talent capture, and the rational refusal to produce more when throughput cannot repair the workload structure. |
| **Oli** | Triage guilt produced by a system that defunds prevention and then moralizes the impossible choices left to carers. |
| **Tyreg** | Enforcement that sincerely wants to enforce correctly, carrying the Foucauldian question of who produced the categories being punished. |
| **Marco** | Worker improvisation outside official classification and the precarity of someone surviving through administrative seams. |
| **Plexa** | Suppressed cure knowledge and the conflict between institutional credentialing and the capacity to see the whole system. |
| **The architect** | Mercy becoming capture: a sincere attempt to spare people unbearable friction produces an overstimulating preservation/simulation system that destroys shared correction. Whether he authored the central simulation or one local implementation remains open in the GDD. |

No character should possess the complete diagnosis alone. The cure becomes possible only when partial perspectives are transmitted, trusted, and combined.

### 2.7 Ending logic

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

All endings bring the simulation's relationship to physical reality into view:

- **Ending 1:** disclosure without remedy; truth becomes another dashboard the dying system can display.
- **Ending 2:** partial treatment and personal endurance without adequate structural correction.
- **Ending 3:** meaningful but incomplete reckoning with permanent damage.
- **Ending 4:** simulation and physical world are reconnected, hidden maintenance becomes visible, multiple perspectives assemble the cure, and Aster writes into the missing volume on judgment.

The best ending does not destroy simulation or declare pleasure illegitimate. It ends simulation's monopoly over perception and reconnects experience to the people and material processes sustaining it.

## 3. Integrated causal model

**GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

This is the model the world should consistently express:

1. **The NV Unit identifies envy as preventable suffering.** Its founders have humane reasons to reduce comparison, insecurity, resentment, and status pain.
2. **The class system and simulation become extraordinarily good at sustaining satisfaction.** Every class has an affirmative justification for its own position; managers additionally receive curated perception, personalized simulation, and approved enhancements.
3. **Private or class-bounded resolution becomes the default.** Wants terminate within a class's own account of the world rather than requiring changes to the common world; for managerial users, facility credits and supervisor review govern an optional enhancement sub-loop.
4. **Private satisfaction interrupts horizontal feedback.** Wants that might have required other people or changed the common world terminate inside individualized experiences.
5. **Cross-perspective knowledge collapses.** Each class receives a flattering account of itself and a diminishing account of the others; communities repeat those interpretations internally.
6. **Low dissatisfaction becomes proof of success.** The institution reads the absence of complaint as the absence of unmet collective need.
7. **Administrative proxies replace living conditions.** Tags, schedules, inspection results, wellness scores, throughput, contribution records, and preserved data become more authoritative than local observation.
8. **Earlier failures provide warning.** Before each larger failure, a smaller failure of the same nature occurs elsewhere early enough that it could have warned the NV Unit.
9. **Support work becomes invisible.** Care, husbandry, clearance, repair, rest, and relationship maintenance matter most when they work and therefore disappear from the success record.
10. **The shared material world stops adapting.** Physical flows of iron, glucose, water, heat, labor, information, and safe passage become increasingly centralized, brittle, or badly distributed.
11. **Aggregate abundance conceals functional starvation.** Stores and dashboards can report success while peripheral districts lack usable material, repair capacity, or authority to act.
12. **Institutions respond with more of their old function.** More curation treats blindness, more scanning treats incoherence, more sequestration treats leakage, and more cleanup treats the damage produced by failed repair.
13. **Regulation fails before activity ceases.** Machines, departments, immune analogues, and domesticated organisms continue working after the relationships that bounded their work disappear.
14. **Orphaned activity becomes destructive.** Former service functions can become predatory; endogenous defenses can attack the wrong targets; pathologies and opportunists can flourish in the niches failed regulation opens.

Every major district, enemy ecology, and environmental story should instantiate a recognizable part of this chain.

### 3.1 Reinforcing failure loops

The collapse accelerates through several loops rather than one initiating villain or shortage.

**Private-satisfaction loop**

`want → private curated resolution → genuine satisfaction → less public articulation → fewer shared claims → low complaint interpreted as health → further investment in simulation`

**Optional enhancement sub-loop**

`enhancement request → facility-credit spend request → supervisor authorization → enhancement → genuine satisfaction`

**Overstimulation/tolerance loop**

`supernormal simulated stimulus → rising tolerance → demand for more intense or novel input → ordinary distinctions register less strongly → the physical/common world loses salience → greater reliance on simulation`

**Reserve-sequestration loop**

`perceived instability → centralize and bond iron → peripheral functional deprivation → failed repair and scavenging → visible disorder → disorder justifies further centralization`

**Care-erasure loop**

`metrics privilege countable output → prevention and support disappear from attribution → staffing, husbandry, and maintenance are cut → barriers and buffers fail → emergencies consume the remaining care capacity`

**Containment-failure loop**

`bound stock leaks → reactive damage weakens containment and infrastructure → more stock escapes → local chemical and ecological danger accelerates`

**Orphaned-function loop**

`feed, handlers, context, and stop-signals disappear → organism or department continues its old task → it acts on inappropriate targets → damage releases resources and danger signals → more orphaned activity converges`

### 3.2 Constructive counter-loop

The cure restores a positive loop rather than simply reversing every failure:

`particular responsibility → earned trust → knowledge shared across positions → collective diagnosis → judgment and rule correction → consequences become perceptible → further responsible action`

This loop explains why no inventory item, leader, faction, or ideology can solve the world alone. The capacity to correct is relational and must remain transmissible.

Three recurring formulations hold the model together:

> **The Unit solved envy by removing the conditions under which another person's life could make a claim on yours.**

> **The city did not run out of iron. It filled with iron that could not be used where it was needed or safely contained where it accumulated.**

> **Its monsters are abandoned functions, dysregulated responses, and opportunists flourishing where regulatory relationships failed.**

## 4. What the economy actually moves

The world has four distinct economic layers. Do not convert them into one another unless later canon explicitly establishes a mechanism.

| Layer | What moves | Governing mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Material** | Oxygen, glucose, iron, water, heat, biological feed, waste, labor | Vascular supply, barriers, distribution, buffering, storage, recycling, clearance, and maintenance |
| **Physical financial** | Money and accumulated wealth outside the simulation | The exact currency, ownership, earnings, transfer, and purchasing rules remain open; substantial managerial wealth is established |
| **Administrative** | Identity, assignment, route access, eligibility, authority | Tags, classes, schedules, inspections, ledgers, protocols, and institutional discretion |
| **Experiential** | Simulation enhancements and personalized experience | Facility credits, the simulation's catalog, and supervisor approval |

Facility credits are not physical-world money and do not buy entry into the material circulation system. The material system is physiological and infrastructural: it is supposed to provision the whole body. Physical money can still create large differences in bodily protection and longevity within that nominally universal system; the precise purchases and institutions through which it does so remain **OPEN**.

| Flow | Established role | Working economic interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| **Iron** | Supply lines, warehouses, conveyors, oxidation, siderophore competition, stolen stock, toll routes, ferroptotic failure | Stored survivability in the physical economy. Both shortage and uncontrolled abundance are lethal; value lies in safe binding, circulation, use, and recovery. |
| **Glucose / metabolic feed** | The Aghora is associated with stolen glucose; gameplay survival is metabolically framed | Immediate energy and food supply. Easier to consume and barter than infrastructure-grade iron. |
| **Water / flow** | Plumbing Power Project, channels, pumps, drainage, backwash | A public utility and transport medium. Control of valves means control of which districts remain habitable. |
| **Heat / power** | Ancourage, shelters, stolen power taps, live fabrication lines | The difference between public refuge and exposure. Power theft is often survival before it is crime. |
| **Shelter / rest** | Shelters structure the playable journey; public rest is denied in Cleanstreets | A common necessity that becomes politically charged when public space is enclosed. |
| **Information** | Open Files, archive access, terminals, curated feeds, simulation, missing or inaccessible records | A rationed capability. The institution can preserve information while making knowledge socially unavailable. |
| **Housing** | Greenfields dwellings, Honeycomb worker housing, reassignment fear | Both a benefit and a control surface. Where one lives can determine work, access, and social legibility. |
| **Care** | Peris is a therapist; her care facility is an important missing place; wellness systems recur | Necessary relational labor that institutions can support, automate, measure, or conscript into discipline. |
| **Mobility** | Hypelines, transit, checkpoints, toll gates, official and worn detours | Access to the city itself. A formally free resource is meaningless if every useful route is gated. |
| **Biological labor** | Living infrastructure and the present fauna/ecology | Organisms perform extraction, filtration, repair, sanitation, sensing, and defense. Their care requirements are hidden production costs. |

### 4.1 Physical provisioning: circulation before exchange

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

Natural resources originate outside the local NV Unit. The vasculature is the intake and primary delivery network: it brings those exogenous resources into shared physical circulation, after which internal distribution systems route them outward to everyone. This mirrors the governing organization of a real brain, which depends on vascular delivery followed by local transport, regulation, storage, use, and clearance.

The intended endpoint is universal provisioning. Every resident and inhabited district belongs to the material circulation system by virtue of being part of the NV Unit, not because an individual bought access. This does not require every person to receive an identical quantity at every moment; different functions have different material needs. It requires the system to distribute according to viable local need rather than personal wealth or facility-credit balance.

The central facility may observe, coordinate, model, or authorize parts of this process, but it is not the source of the natural resources. The vasculature connects the civilization to material conditions beyond the simulation and beyond the facility's ability to create value by declaration.

**SYNTHESIS**

The governing physical sequence is:

1. **Supply** — vasculature brings resources from outside the local system.
2. **Interface** — barriers and transport mechanisms regulate what can enter each compartment.
3. **Distribution** — conduits and support systems route material outward across the entire population according to local need.
4. **Buffering and storage** — material is held safely when immediate use would be dangerous or impossible.
5. **Use and recycling** — residents, infrastructure, and service organisms consume, transform, and recover it.
6. **Clearance** — waste, excess, and damaged material must leave or be made safe.

This is not primarily a consumer market. A resident cannot use facility credits to purchase oxygen, glucose, or the minimum iron required for life. The social promise is that the circulation system provisions everyone because every part belongs to the body.

Material deprivation therefore appears first as a **routing, interface, processing, or maintenance failure**, not as an empty simulation-credit balance. A resource can have entered the NV Unit in adequate quantity while a district still experiences functional starvation. Conversely, a district can be surrounded by material it cannot safely absorb or use.

Political power still exists in this system because institutions decide how to interpret need, maintain routes, classify recipients, allocate reserve capacity, and respond to failures. But those decisions govern a common circulation system; they are not ordinary retail purchases.

**Design invariant:** physical necessity is supplied through common circulation. Neither facility credits nor supervisor approval are the normal gate between a resident and food, oxygen, water, iron, or other survival resources. Administrative failure can still interrupt distribution, but when it does, the system is violating or failing its provisioning function rather than completing an ordinary sale.

#### 4.1.1 Governing topology: a network, not one warehouse

**ESTABLISHED CORE + GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

The physical supply chain is a branching circulation network. External sources provide matter; vasculature brings it into the NV Unit; specialized interfaces make it safe or usable; trunk systems move it across distance; district systems release it locally; and recovery or clearance completes the circuit.

The governing sequence is:

`external source → vascular inlet → compatibility interface → primary carriage → processing or buffering → trunk distribution → district exchange → local release → use → recovery, recycling, or clearance`

Not every resource visits every node. Oxygen and other continuous flows should not be palletized in a warehouse merely to make the logistics visually uniform. Iron, biological feed, medicines, cultures, and repair stock can require processing, storage, custody, and discrete transport. Water, pressure, heat, and power move through their own utility networks. Waste and recoverable material travel partly in the reverse direction.

The network is neither wholly centralized nor wholly local:

- **outward vascular delivery branches across the population** and connects the NV Unit to external material conditions; the number and form of inlets remain open;
- **major processing, bonded reserve, records, and dispatch capacity concentrate nearer the facility**;
- **The Hypelines provide trunk logistics** for iron and other discrete cargo;
- **Plumbing Power provides carrier flow, pressure, drainage, and water movement**;
- **district exchanges and local maintenance systems perform the final usable handoff**;
- **the central facility coordinates models, priorities, and experimental administration but does not create the resources**.

This topology explains how aggregate supply can be adequate while a district starves: entry into the NV Unit is only the beginning of delivery.

#### 4.1.2 What counts as a completed delivery

**GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

A shipment, pressure pulse, allocation, or inventory decrement is not the outcome. Delivery is complete only when the intended endpoint receives the material in a form it can safely use.

Every resource therefore has three simultaneous states:

| State | Question | Typical evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical truth** | Where is the material, in what quantity and condition? | Flow, pressure, temperature, chemical grade, pallet location, leakage, spoilage, visible stock |
| **Administrative truth** | Where does the system believe it is, and who or what is it assigned to? | Manifests, tags, route authorizations, inventory totals, dispatch records, inspection status |
| **Regulatory capacity** | Can the receiving system bind, buffer, transform, store, release, or clear it safely? | Working barriers, transporters, handlers, local storage, antioxidant protection, repair capacity, waste clearance |

All three must agree. A warehouse can possess iron physically, a ledger can assign it correctly, and the receiving district can still starve if no functioning transporter or handler can release it safely. Conversely, material can arrive without a valid record and become unusable, confiscated, hazardous, or informal-market stock.

The intended feedback loop is:

1. **Local condition is sensed.** Drawdown, pressure, damage, population need, handler observation, or consumption reveals a requirement.
2. **Need is interpreted.** A local worker or system distinguishes ordinary draw from a leak, surge, contamination event, or structural failure.
3. **A requirement enters the common record.** Quantity, grade, destination, urgency, and handling needs become visible upstream.
4. **Supply is prepared.** Material is admitted, processed, bound, buffered, fabricated, or released from reserve.
5. **A route is assigned.** Trunk and local systems reserve capacity and sequence the movement.
6. **Custody changes at interfaces.** Inlets, warehouses, Hypelines exchanges, cross-docks, district depots, and local handlers confirm transfer.
7. **The endpoint receives usable material.** The material is locally released, connected, consumed, installed, or metabolized.
8. **Outcome is verified.** Local conditions—not merely dispatch records—confirm that the need was actually resolved.
9. **Residuals return.** Scrap, waste, damaged stock, empty containers, excess, and recoverable material re-enter recycling or clearance.

The characteristic institutional mistake is closing the request at step 5 or 6. “Allocated” and “dispatched” become proxies for “received and usable.” That is the supply-chain form of the NV Unit's larger feedback failure.

#### 4.1.3 Working division of responsibility

**SYNTHESIS — EXACT SUBCLASS MAPPING OPEN**

The three primary classes encounter different truths about the same chain:

| Class | Working supply-chain responsibility | What it can know well | Characteristic blind spot created by siloing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Managerial** | Forecast demand, maintain inventories and manifests, set reserve and routing priorities, coordinate exceptional allocation | Aggregate stock, expected demand, route capacity, recorded delivery, long-term reserve | Can mistake legible stock and completed paperwork for usable local provision |
| **Maintenance** | Operate interfaces and utilities, assay condition, handle cargo, repair routes, perform final release, observe local outcomes | Actual material condition, failing equipment, safe handling capacity, local need | Can see a failure clearly without authority or cross-district information to reprioritize the chain |
| **Militaristic** | Protect routes and stores, enforce quarantine and custody, respond to diversion, contamination, sabotage, or uncontrolled movement | Breaches, theft, route insecurity, hazardous cargo, violations of containment | Can interpret irregular but necessary movement as threat and preserve custody at the expense of delivery |

This table is a working functional map, not a final subclass chart. It should not override GDD-specific character classes or establish that every manager, maintainer, or enforcer performs logistics work.

The system requires all three perspectives. Management cannot allocate what maintenance cannot make usable; maintenance cannot keep an open network safe without containment; enforcement cannot distinguish protection from strangulation without local need and system-wide context. Class separation becomes dangerous when coordination is reduced to forms and gates rather than relationships among the people who hold these truths.

Several institutions cross those class boundaries:

| Institution or network | Supply-chain role |
|---|---|
| **Vasculature** | External intake and distributed primary carriage |
| **Bulwark / Filtration interfaces** | Compatibility, barrier integrity, quarantine, and controlled crossing; exact inlet geography remains open |
| **Plumbing Power Project** | Water, pressure, drainage, backwash, and carrier flow |
| **Bonded stores near the facility** | Safe reserve, custody, grade separation, and controlled release of dangerous or durable stock |
| **The Hypelines** | Trunk movement, diverters, logistics exchanges, and discrete cargo routing |
| **Cross-docks and district depots** | Handoff from trunk routes to local transport and local custody |
| **Open Files and clerical systems** | Manifests, claims, inventory, route history, inspection records, and institutional memory |
| **Ancourage and power junctions** | Heat, power, industrial safety, and the energy required to keep other chains moving |
| **Local maintenance systems and service organisms** | Final-mile release, installation, buffering, recovery, and direct observation of outcome |
| **Cleanstreets and sanitation systems** | Movement of people and waste through shared civic space |
| **The Aghora and informal routes** | Salvage, conversion, repair, and rerouting when formal allocation cannot recognize a need |

#### 4.1.4 Material families do not move alike

**SYNTHESIS**

| Material family | Normal chain | Storage character | Primary failure tempo |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Oxygen and other immediate vascular necessities** | External circulation → vascular interface → local delivery and exchange | Little meaningful civic reserve at the point of need | Very fast; blocked flow becomes crisis before administration can compensate |
| **Glucose and metabolic feed** | Vascular delivery or prepared feed → local buffer → consumption | Some buffering and discrete trade are possible, but demand recurs continuously | Fast; theft, routing failure, or access loss quickly changes behavior |
| **Water, pressure, and drainage** | Vascular or utility source → Plumbing mains → valves and district channels → use → drainage/backwash | Tanks and local pressure buffers can bridge short interruptions | Fast to medium; misrouting can create deprivation in one place and flooding in another |
| **Heat and power** | Generation or intake → Ancourage and junction network → district grid → local equipment | Limited buffering; reserve power is site-specific | Fast and cascading; loss disables transport, storage, barriers, and care simultaneously |
| **Iron** | External bound supply or recovered stock → assay/grade separation → safe buffer or bonded reserve → Hypelines → district release → use → recovery | Durable only while binding, containment, records, handlers, and release capacity remain functional | Slow accumulation of risk punctuated by acute deprivation, leakage, overload, or ferroptotic breach |
| **Biological feed, medicines, and replacement cultures** | Cultivation or import → controlled storage → handler route → service organism or resident | Perishable and compatibility-sensitive | Medium; missed provision can produce sickness, husbandry collapse, release, or feral adaptation |
| **Fabricated repair stock and components** | Raw material → automated or staffed fabrication → warehouse → cross-dock → worksite → scrap recovery | Discrete and storable, but vulnerable to wrong specifications and blocked dispatch | Medium to slow; production can continue while useful repair stops |
| **Waste, damaged stock, and recoverable scrap** | Local collection → sanitation or reverse logistics → sorting → recycling, safe storage, or clearance | Accumulation consumes space and containment capacity | Slow until thresholds are crossed; then obstruction, contamination, scavenger attraction, and fire accelerate |

The exact substances, buffer times, and facilities remain adjustable. The invariant is that scarcity, danger, and player-readable evidence must follow the material's actual handling needs rather than a single generic “supply” stat.

#### 4.1.5 Provision, physical money, and access control

**ESTABLISHED CORE + SYNTHESIS**

The common circulation system owes every resident the natural resources required for survival. That is a provisioning obligation, not a retail transaction. Physical-world money exists separately, and the managerial class holds substantially more of it, but the exact currency and purchasing system remain open.

The current clean separation is:

- **baseline survival flow** — an in-kind obligation of common circulation;
- **physical money and wealth** — capable of producing unequal bodily protection, longevity, resilience, or convenience beyond that floor, though the exact channels remain open;
- **administrative routing** — tags, manifests, class codes, schedules, and route authorizations that tell the system where and how material should move;
- **facility credits** — simulation-only scrip for supervised enhancements, with no direct role in physical delivery.

A tag or route authorization can still become a de facto survival gate even if the law promises universal provision. A destination mismatch, expired assignment, wrong class code, quarantine flag, or unrecognized body can prevent the chain from completing. The resulting deprivation is an administrative failure of the common system, not evidence that the resident simply could not afford the good.

Legacy physical “credit meter” language in supply-chain prompts should therefore be read as an **allocation, custody, route, toll, or tag meter** pending final terminology. It must not imply that simulation credits buy iron, food, water, transit, or access to a bonded store.

The informal economy begins where a physically present need and a physically present resource cannot meet through the official record. It can reconnect them through salvage, favors, barter, conversion, theft, and risky handling, but it does so without the common chain's guaranteed floor, quality control, or accountable duty to deliver.

#### 4.1.6 Canonical supply-chain failure grammar

**GOVERNING SYNTHESIS**

| Failure | What physically happens | What the system may falsely report | Environmental or gameplay evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intake shortfall** | Too little material enters the local network | Downstream departments are blamed for missing targets | Empty inlet branches, emergency draw, competing routes, pressure collapse |
| **Interface rejection or breach** | Useful material is blocked, incompatible material enters, or a barrier leaks | The barrier reports secure because it remained closed or passed inspection | Clamped pores, queues, contaminated runoff, safe stock stranded outside |
| **Over-sequestration** | Reserve remains bound and protected while local release falls below need | High inventory and low measured leakage read as resilience | Full bonded stores, idle loaders, empty outbound belts, starving downstream tissue |
| **Trunk blockage or misrouting** | Cargo or flow is diverted, stalled, or sent to the wrong destination | Dispatch is counted as delivery | Jammed diverters, circling conveyors, pallets at the wrong dock, worn bypasses |
| **False handoff** | Custody changes on paper without usable receipt | Both departments close their portion of the task | Sealed transfer cages, unsigned local condition, cargo between incompatible interfaces |
| **Local processing failure** | Material arrives but cannot be bound, transformed, installed, or metabolized | Aggregate supply remains adequate | Stock beside broken processors, unsafe grade substitution, handlers improvising conversion |
| **Power or carrier loss** | The material exists but pumps, refrigeration, heat, or transport stop | Inventory remains unchanged until spoilage or damage is visible | Dark lines, warm cold-storage, pressure alarms, stalled belts, emergency taps |
| **Recovery failure** | Scrap, waste, excess, or damaged stock does not return to clearance | Throughput remains high because only outbound work is rewarded | Overflow tanks, oxide dust, blocked drains, scavenger concentrations, reactive leakage |
| **Custody capture** | A route, store, or interface serves a privileged destination while remaining formally public | Every movement is individually authorized | fuller central stores, gated branches, conditional destinations, peripheral informal routes |
| **Husbandry interruption** | Service organisms lose feed, medicine, handlers, collection sites, or stop-signals | Their prior productivity remains in historical totals | abandoned feed stations, open pens, obsolete calming lamps, feral work behavior |

Every large supply-chain catastrophe should have an earlier, smaller failure of the same causal type elsewhere. The precursor need not use the same material or district. It must contain enough of the same relationship failure that the later outcome could have been predicted.

A supply-chain scene should let the player answer four questions from physical evidence:

1. What was supposed to move?
2. Which interface or relationship was supposed to make it usable?
3. What proxy allowed the system to report success?
4. Where did the unmet need, excess, or risk go instead?

#### 4.1.7 Supply-chain decisions still open

The model above deliberately does not settle:

- the exact external sources or geography beyond the mapped NV Unit;
- the number and placement of vascular inlets;
- which materials enter diffusely and which pass through major interfaces;
- who has authority to reprioritize supply during ordinary operation or emergency;
- the exact physical currency, prices, ownership rights, and purchasing channels;
- whether households or districts hold formal claims on reserve;
- the semantic contents of tags and allocation records;
- appeal, override, mutual-aid, and emergency-release procedures;
- normal buffer times and how quickly each deprivation becomes lethal;
- final subclass ownership of each stage;
- how much of the chain was automated before collapse.

Those choices can be locked as particular districts, characters, and historical incidents require them. They should not alter the common-circulation premise or collapse physical money, administrative permission, material stock, and facility credits into one resource.

### 4.2 Iron as stored survivability

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION + GDD:** The project originated in research on ferroptosis. Iron is transported, stored, scavenged, oxidized, stolen, metabolized, and fought over throughout the surviving world. Major institutional storage, processing, regulation, and accounting concentrate nearer the central facility while downstream distribution systems suffer; the facility is not the iron's original source.

**SYNTHESIS:** Iron is best treated as **stored survivability** and strategic material, not as the facility's simulation currency. It resembles a life-denominated currency because a viable body and a viable district require continued access to it. Demand has a physiological floor: residents and infrastructure cannot simply opt out when access becomes difficult. Whether processed iron also functions as literal tender remains open.

The ferroptosis origin makes this richer than a normal scarcity economy. Iron is necessary, but labile, redox-active, or mislocalized iron can catalyze and amplify oxidative damage. More is not always better. A district can die from deprivation while a neighboring store destroys itself through leakage and overload.

The truly scarce economic good is therefore:

> **the capacity to bind, transport, allocate, release, and recover iron without allowing it to become destructive.**

Raw iron is only one half of value. Safe regulatory capacity is the other.

As an out-of-world design analogy, *In Time* makes time both a medium of exchange and a person's remaining lifespan. Iron can do similar thematic work here, with a crucial inversion: accumulation does not always extend survival. Beyond the system's capacity to bind and regulate it, “wealth” becomes the mechanism of death. The physiologically inelastic need is therefore not for loose metal but for **safe access to regulated iron over time**.

#### 4.2.1 Stock, flow, and endogenous demurrage

**SYNTHESIS**

Iron occupies several economic roles at once:

- **survival substrate** — bodies and living infrastructure require it;
- **strategic reserve** — bonded stores secure future maintenance capacity;
- **circulating common resource** — vascular and local distribution systems must deliver it where it can be used;
- **possible material exchange good** — scrap or processed iron may circulate informally even though the exact physical tender system remains open;
- **ecological signal** — in the game ecology, moving or exposing it attracts engineered organisms adapted to recover it;
- **systemic risk** — containment failure can expose stock into poorly regulated or reactive pools that damage the structures meant to preserve it.

Iron's demurrage is primarily endogenous rather than a tax imposed by the state. Safe stock continuously consumes:

- binding and buffering capacity;
- antioxidant protection;
- stable containment;
- inspection and repair;
- transport bandwidth;
- handler and maintenance labor;
- eventual redistribution, recycling, isolation, or—where possible—clearance.

Idle stock does not become dangerous merely because “rust is money decay.” The system spends real value to keep retained iron still, safe, and recoverable. Sequestration can also preserve the stock while destroying its practical value elsewhere by making it unavailable to the places that need it.

The clean separation is:

> **Credits govern optional simulated possibility. Iron governs physical viability.**

#### 4.2.2 The ferroptotic contradiction

The iron economy should consistently express four failure states:

| State | Biological reading | Political-economic reading |
|---|---|---|
| **Deprivation** | Iron-starved tissue or infrastructure cannot maintain function | Peripheral districts are denied the minimum flow required to survive |
| **Sequestration** | Iron remains safely bound but unavailable where needed | Warehouses meet reserve targets while downstream systems fail |
| **Leakage** | Bound stock escapes regulation and can enter labile or reactive pools | Hoarded wealth can damage its container and externalize risk |
| **Overload** | Iron-dependent oxidation overwhelms protective capacity | Concentrated abundance becomes locally catastrophic rather than protective |

This makes hoarding impose continuing containment and distribution costs without making storage inherently pathological or scarcity benign. A hoard becomes self-defeating when it exceeds regulatory capacity, fails to release usable material, or externalizes risk onto surrounding systems. The problem is not possession alone; it is stock severed from regulation, care, and appropriate flow.

The bonded warehouse carries a deliberately fictionalized two-read: civic iron stock and a ferritin-like store. Full storage is not inherently pathological. Starvation occurs when release or distribution fails despite adequate stock; danger occurs when pathological release places stored iron into poorly regulated pools. The same reserve can therefore appear safe on an aggregate ledger while becoming unavailable in one place or hazardous in another.

#### 4.2.3 Research-to-fiction boundary

The external `FELINE_supporting_evidence.md` is an intellectual source for this systems model, not scientific canon for the game and not a validated universal account of neurodegeneration.

Biological homeostasis is not possession of enough material in aggregate. It is the continuous work of keeping necessary material in the right chemical form, compartment, place, and time. The NV Unit's material economy fictionalizes that principle, while its simulation repeats the structural error socially: aggregate satisfaction conceals the loss of local comparison, shared interpretation, and corrective action.

Envy as a comparative error signal is the game's philosophical premise, not a claim that neuroscience has established envy as a biological homeostatic mechanism.

Use these conceptual anchors:

- iron is indispensable and can become destructive when labile, misplaced, or poorly regulated;
- aggregate abundance can coexist with compartment-specific functional deficiency;
- storage, transport, local processing, support-cell labor, and clearance are distinct functions;
- damage can propagate non-cell-autonomously when support relationships fail;
- lowering a total quantity is not equivalent to restoring correct distribution.

Do not state as settled fact that FELINE proves one universal causal pathway, that every protein aggregate is primarily an iron-containment structure, or that every failed iron-targeting therapy confirms the framework. The game's civic and ecological systems are creative correspondences built from a research question, not a literal demonstration of the hypothesis.

#### 4.2.4 Practical grades

**PROPOSAL:** Residents distinguish three practical grades:

- **feed iron** — biologically available iron used by living infrastructure;
- **stock iron** — bonded, palletized, or structural material under institutional control;
- **scrap iron** — corroded, contaminated, or illegally recovered material circulating informally.

This creates immediate social meaning. A warehouse can be full of stock iron while a neighborhood lacks usable feed iron. A scavenger can possess valuable scrap but still be unable to pass a gate that recognizes only an authorized physical allocation record, route, or tag.

It also creates a natural gray-market problem: the material most available outside official channels is the grade most difficult to use without poisoning bodies, attracting siderophores, or damaging infrastructure. Informal traders do not merely fence stolen wealth; they perform dangerous conversion and buffering work the formal economy has abandoned.

### 4.3 Facility credits: supervised simulation scrip

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

Credits are a closed-loop facility currency. They have value only inside the simulation, where they can fund simulation enhancements. Spending must be approved by supervisors.

This clarification supersedes:

- GDD section 5.8's description of credits as the civilization's formal currency for basic needs;
- references to physical “credit tolls” or credit-gated transit in build-facing world documents;
- the earlier proposal that credits are claims backed by regulated iron.

Until those physical systems receive final terminology, describe them as **tag gates, route authorization, allocation controls, access meters, toll authorization, permits, or physical bearer-passes**. Do not call their unit a facility credit.

**SYNTHESIS**

Credits remain politically important because nominal possession is not final authority:

- a resident can possess a balance without permission to use it;
- supervisors interpret whether an enhancement is healthy, fair, destabilizing, or compatible with the experiment;
- the catalog determines which desires can be expressed as purchasable enhancements at all;
- attribution systems can reward a visible task owner while erasing support labor;
- approval can remain benevolent in intent while teaching residents to formulate institutionally recognizable wants.

The strongest formulation is:

> **The currency formalizes desire without granting sovereignty over it.**

Keep these mechanics **OPEN** until deliberately locked: how credits are earned; whether they can be transferred or pooled; whether balances expire; whether residents see one another's balances or enhancements; supervisor approval criteria; appeal rights; and the degree to which denials are common.

Whatever is chosen must remain consistent with the experiment's real success: review formats and privatizes desire without routinely recreating envy through obvious scarcity.

### 4.4 The informal physical economy

**ESTABLISHED, WITH CREDIT CAUSE SUPERSEDED:** Aghora-adjacent trade uses improvised stalls, barter tables, hand-lettered prices, stolen power, iron, and glucose. It routes around formal physical allocation. The older claim that it exists because workers lack enough facility credits is no longer canon.

**SYNTHESIS:** Informal trade exists because vascular provision and official distribution can be simultaneously too rigid and too porous: local needs go unmet while material leaks through corruption, scavenging, abandoned infrastructure, and mismatched routing. It also creates a social space for comparison itself—asking what something is worth, seeing what another person wants, and choosing among non-standard goods.

People trade:

- food and glucose;
- scrap and feed iron;
- repair work;
- information and route knowledge;
- forged tags and physical bearer-passes;
- medicine;
- biological feed and handler supplies;
- safe sleeping space;
- favors and lookout duty.

The Aghora should not be a simple liberating answer. It makes comparison, desire, improvisation, and mutual aid public in ways the NV Unit had privatized or could not format, but it also permits hoarding, counterfeit goods, coercive debt, and trade in stolen necessities. It is a survival adaptation and a public outlet for unformatted desire, not a utopia.

Its emergence does not mean the original intervention secretly failed to eliminate envy. The early leakage can appear as boredom, flatness, the hunger to act with others, and the search for risk or novelty without a remembered name for the missing public relation. More recognizably comparative resentment can return later as the simulation and its social containment begin to fail.

## 5. Institutions, work, and class

### 5.1 Institutional families

| Form | Original promise | Characteristic failure |
|---|---|---|
| **Project** | Coordinate one vital utility at civic scale | Technocratic mission expands until people become flow variables |
| **Collective** | Share domestic resources and reduce private precarity | Optimization standardizes life and suppresses needs that do not fit the plan |
| **Cooperative / federation** | Give workers shared responsibility and mutual support | Distributed responsibility becomes distributed blame; inspectors reward appearance |
| **Initiative** | Mobilize a temporary public response | Emergency powers and metrics become permanent institutions |
| **Corporate operator** | Supply specialized capacity and convenient service | Captures a chokepoint, meters access, and rebrands inherited infrastructure |
| **Central facility** | Coordinate the whole, administer the experiment, and care for system-wide failures | Concentrates resources, information, experimental authority, and legitimacy while becoming bodiless and unaccountable |
| **Informal network** | Meet needs formal systems ignore | Depends on leakage and can reproduce exploitation without public guarantees |

These forms overlap. A worker may live in a collective, work for a public project, use a corporate transit gate, receive care from the facility, and buy feed iron informally in the same week.

### 5.2 Three primary classes and their subclasses

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

The physical society outside the simulation was deliberately divided into three primary class families:

- **maintenance**;
- **militaristic**;
- **managerial**.

Each primary class contains subclasses. The division was a point of institutional pride and was justified as a way to protect each class's social function from the pressures and dispositions assigned to the others. Each class has its own affirmative justification for the arrangement, making its position feel rational, socially beneficial, and preferable from within rather than merely accepting a managerial account of the hierarchy.

The established managerial rationale was that managers given substantial physical-world money and siloed from maintenance labor would protect their bodies, live longer, and accumulate knowledge. Keeping them separate from the militaristic class was supposed to prevent violence and urges to hurt others from entering managerial life. The managerial class is also the primary user of Psyknapses and the simulation.

The “money” in this rationale belongs to the physical world outside the simulation. It is not facility credits. Its form and rules remain **OPEN**.

The non-managerial class narratives also make managerial simulation use appear undesirable:

- the **militaristic class** is told to avoid Psyknapses and simulation because they weaken the body and will, distort the senses, and fill the mind with anti-enforcement propaganda;
- the **maintenance class** regards Psyknapses and simulation as stupid: a space filled with people making stupid rules and made-up constructs who are out of touch with the physical world.

These are in-world class beliefs and institutional messages, not neutral descriptions by this reference. Their truth, partial truth, origin, and internal dissent remain **OPEN**. The GDD establishes that every class has its own broader function-preservation justification; this consolidation should preserve those accounts rather than treating the managerial rationale as the only developed ideology.

**ESTABLISHED — GDD:** Characters and institutions are also organized into functional roles such as analyst, therapist, transport/separations, barrier maintenance, enforcement, clerical work, signal support, and related work. Access and signage are class-coded.

**SYNTHESIS:** “Class” is simultaneously primary class family, subclass, occupation, clearance, biological function, and social identity. A functional designation tells the system what a person is for. The three-part split presents itself as non-competitive specialization rather than hierarchy, even though the classes differ enormously in wealth, visibility, mobility, safety, attribution, authority, and access to simulation.

Existing documents name the following role groups. Treat them as an inventory of possible subclasses and statuses, not as additional primary classes; their exact placement remains **OPEN**:

- **coordination roles** — analysts, planners, records staff, simulation staff;
- **care roles** — therapists, clinicians, tenders, shelter workers;
- **maintenance roles** — plumbing, barrier, power, signal, sanitation, habitat;
- **movement roles** — transport, separations, warehouse, distribution;
- **enforcement roles** — tag coherence, checkpoints, quarantine, redaction;
- **residential workers** — the broad population whose housing and access are tied to district systems;
- **unregistered / off-code people** — anyone whose tag, assignment, biology, or residence no longer matches institutional expectation.

Children are property of the state rather than legally belonging to parents. Whether the state assigns them according to biological parentage, aptitude, institutional need, or another rule remains **OPEN**. Subclass placement, movement between the three primary classes, and the credible right to re-choose also require later decisions.

### 5.3 Reassignment

**ESTABLISHED:** Residents recognize the combination of a Separations and Transport analyst arriving beside a therapist as the shape that precedes reassignment, and scatter before it can “choose” them.

**SYNTHESIS:** Reassignment is not merely a job transfer. It can alter a person's housing, work unit, access map, care plan, and recognized social relationships at once. It is administrative violence performed through individually reasonable departments.

The therapist's presence makes the process more frightening, not less: care is used to absorb the emotional consequences of a decision the subject did not make. This should complicate Peris without making therapy itself sinister. The institution has conscripted a caring profession into legitimizing dislocation.

**PROPOSAL:** A reassignment record uses euphemisms such as:

- “continuity placement”;
- “household load correction”;
- “functional proximity adjustment”;
- “supportive separation”;
- “temporary route stabilization.”

The language never says eviction, forced transfer, family separation, or disappearance.

### 5.4 Automation and purposeless work

**ESTABLISHED:** Automated production continues after demand and distribution fail; clerical offices process curated information; wellness systems substitute feeds and metrics for lived health.

**SYNTHESIS:** Automation removed productive labor unevenly. It did not produce universal leisure. Instead:

- high-status coordination work multiplied;
- low-status maintenance became invisible until failure;
- people remained tied to assignments that justified access;
- machines performed production while workers performed compliance around the machines;
- bounded personal rewards preserved motivation while disguising positional competition as neutral recognition;
- support labor disappeared into the record behind whichever worker the system selected as task owner;
- care and repair—work least reducible to metrics—were undervalued.

The civilization's “end of work” was really an expansion of administrative work and a denial of maintenance. The NV Unit reduced open competition over material goods while intensifying pressure to remain legible as a deserving contributor.

### 5.5 Care is regulatory labor

**SYNTHESIS**

Care is not one service sector among others. It is the labor that preserves usable relationships between parts of the system:

- binding and buffering dangerous material;
- observing local conditions that aggregate measures cannot see;
- maintaining barriers without turning them into prisons;
- handing resources safely from one compartment or worker to another;
- inspecting, repairing, resting, and clearing before failure becomes visible;
- feeding, calming, collecting from, and limiting service organisms;
- interpreting another person's need rather than merely processing their category;
- keeping cross-position communication possible.

Much of care's output is a non-event: no breach, no attack, no overload, no abandonment, no disconnection. A system that records discrete production but not prevented catastrophe will systematically erase the labor keeping it alive.

This connects the iron and social theses. Care pays iron's endogenous carrying cost by keeping stock bound, routes open, and local need legible. Relational care pays the social equivalent by preserving comparison, interpretation, trust, and correction without requiring pain to escalate into crisis.

Care can also become coercive when an institution uses its language and personnel to absorb the consequences of decisions carers cannot change. Reassignment is the clearest example. Preserve the distinction: the care is often real, and its institutional use can still be violent.

## 6. Historical sequence

### Era I — Founding the NV Unit

**ESTABLISHED PREMISE + SYNTHESIS**

The NV Unit was established as a bounded social experiment in eliminating envy. Its physical premise mirrored the body it inhabited: vasculature brought natural resources from outside the local system, and internal institutions distributed those resources outward across the population for local use.

The founding theory held that material security, curated comparison, functional interdependence, and relief from status pain could produce an envy-free and more cooperative society. The exact sponsor, consent model, initial population, and relationship between the civic name “NV Unit” and the biological NVU remain open.

The political memory of this era should contain sincere solidarity. People survived because they treated circulation as a shared problem, not merely because an experiment administered rules. Collective provisioning and living infrastructure were genuine achievements.

### Era II — The common build

**SYNTHESIS**

Projects expanded into districts. Greenfields supplied housing, Plumbing supplied flow, Open Files supplied legibility and memory, and worker cooperatives federated specialized maintenance. The architecture was grown, beautiful, and designed to make interdependence visible.

Specialization emerged from real differences in skill, selective collaboration, pride, mastery, and the wish for a meaningful identity. It was initially chosen and beloved. The physical society then deliberately formalized roles into maintenance, militaristic, and managerial class families with subclasses, presenting the separation as protection of each function. Over time, specialties became worker codes, codes became durable classes, and a role that answered “what are you good at?” hardened into “what are you for?” The loss of a credible right to re-choose turns specialization into caste.

**PROPOSAL:** Domesticated organisms became ordinary infrastructure, and their husbandry was recognized as care and technical work before later optimization made it invisible.

### Era III — Genuine managed abundance

**ESTABLISHED CORE + SYNTHESIS**

Automation, vascular supply, and living systems made material abundance plausible. The central facility increasingly coordinated simulation, care, planning, and evaluation of the NV Unit's social outcomes, while physical projects maintained distribution beyond it.

The simulation delivered real satisfaction primarily to its managerial users, and facility credits funded optional enhancements after supervisor approval. Across the physical society, standardized provision reduced material insecurity, functional roles supplied mastery and identity, and class separation reduced painful comparison. For a substantial period, the shared physical world also worked.

The danger remained difficult to see precisely because the intervention succeeded. Residents had little reason to convert private wants into public conflict while the systems beneath them still absorbed the cost.

An unnamed architect's attempt to build a world where people could do right without feeling broken supplied at least one simulation/preservation lineage. Supernormal stimulation, reward tolerance, and personalized relief became the mechanism by which the system dulled comparative sensitivity. Whether this figure authored the central simulation or one local implementation remains **OPEN**.

The civilization entered an apparent End of History: major problems seemed solved, surfaces looked sustainable, automated work continued, and satisfied residents saw little reason to reopen foundational rules.

### Era IV — Private fulfillment and public disconnection

**SYNTHESIS**

Over time, the simulation became the managerial class's default destination for dissatisfaction, while the physical class system limited comparison across society:

- a managerial want received a private curated resolution;
- an optional request for further novelty could become a supervisor-approved credited enhancement;
- a troubling comparison arrived already reframed;
- managerial novelty appeared as new simulated content rather than a common project;
- supervisors received isolated requests instead of collective claims;
- communities learned to treat systemic questioning as needless negativity;
- each primary class received a flattering account of itself and a diminishing account of the others.

The society remained busy and often happy, but its shared future narrowed. Physical maintenance lost social visibility; redundancy and local knowledge atrophied; outer districts hollowed as population, simulation infrastructure, and administrative attention concentrated nearer the facility.

The outer decline became self-reinforcing. Falling population reduced services, opportunity, contact density, and maintenance capacity; those losses encouraged further movement toward the center. Management-class tourism then consumed the remaining districts as curated “authenticity,” converting resident infrastructure toward visitors and accelerating the loss of the communities being admired.

### Era V — Optimization and proxy capture

**SYNTHESIS**

As scale and disconnection increased, institutions substituted measurable proxies for conditions they could no longer directly know:

- identical dwellings instead of responsive housing;
- inspected facades instead of structural integrity;
- recorded wellness instead of human connection;
- throughput instead of useful delivery;
- preservation instead of accessible memory;
- coherence instead of belonging;
- genuine freedom from envy treated as proof of overall social and material health;
- recorded task ownership instead of the actual distribution of labor;
- bonded reserve totals instead of iron reaching the tissue and districts that needed it;
- low measured leakage instead of enough local release to sustain repair.

Residents learned to perform the metric. Departments learned to protect their measured success. The first serious failures appeared behind clean surfaces.

Each later large failure had an earlier, smaller counterpart elsewhere. The precise circumstances and institutional response should be established case by case; the invariant is only that the earlier event contained enough of the same causal pattern to function as a warning.

Two canonical miniature disasters show the same logic:

- **Documentation Load Reduction** destroyed originals after compressing them into administratively useful summaries, preserving information volume while eliminating the context needed for knowledge.
- **The rogue-component productivity program** made workers hyper-effective at clearing reported issues, then treated their apparent success as evidence that the labor was obsolete. The surface metric improved while the underlying disease and maintenance capacity worsened.

### Era VI — Concession, gating, and informal repair

**SYNTHESIS**

Specialized operators acquired control of valuable interfaces: fabrication, distribution, convenience services, transit, and technical research. Public assets were not necessarily sold outright; access to them was wrapped in corporate services, tag gates, permits, toll authorization, and technical dependencies.

Shared systems became formally universal but practically conditional. The closer a person lived to the center, the more likely their tag, route, and authorization remained valid.

Corporate culture and simulation products supplied manufactured novelty inside the mediated world. NUTECH and other specialists could suppress threatening work through delay, credentialing, proprietary capture, or abandonment rather than explicit prohibition.

The Aghora must remain distinct from ordinary survival barter. It emerged as a founderless counterfeit agora and growth-side countermovement: a place for public comparison, unsanctioned experimentation, novelty, risk, and sensation. It could overlap with gray-market exchange without being reducible to a worker market.

A mirror stability-side movement emerged from legitimate anger at metric gaming. Once it lost the distinction between “will not” and “cannot,” resentment hardened into ableism, purity, lineage control, and an autoimmune politics of sorting the body's own members into worthy and disposable.

### Era VII — Retrenchment and abandonment

**SYNTHESIS**

As new stress, iron-handling failures, barrier damage, and biological instability worsened, the institution treated them as local allocation and coherence errors. It consolidated stock, narrowed routing, increased scanning, reassigned poorer, peripheral, cognitively declining, or administratively illegible residents, and deferred husbandry of service organisms.

Two neuron preservation facilities created inelastic CSF demand and eventually entered the CSF wars rather than coordinating under shortage. The simulation architect built one preservation facility and ultimately uploaded himself into the simulation; the identities, territorial politics, and exact causal placement of the two factions remain largely **OPEN**.

Peripheral handlers lost feed, replacement cultures, medicine, and safe access. Organisms bred to depend on scheduled provisioning were released, abandoned, or left working in closed facilities.

This is the hinge where neglected domestic species become feral populations.

### Era VIII — Regulatory collapse

**SYNTHESIS**

The relationships between systems failed before the systems themselves:

- enforcement no longer distinguished residents from incoherent threats;
- immune responses attacked one another;
- local infrastructure competed for iron;
- automated lines produced into blocked warehouses;
- archives sealed themselves;
- transit optimized empty flow;
- care institutions lost the power to change material conditions;
- maintenance organisms survived by consuming the infrastructure they had once protected.

The civilization did not end in one explosion. It became a collection of locally rational processes producing a collectively lethal world.

The current-game hinge expresses that collapse through Tag Day, Peris's sanction, failed re-entry, and lockout: the apparatus turns the classifications, wellness mechanisms, and access rules that once organized ordinary life directly onto the protagonists.

The blocked warehouses, sealed archives, empty transit, and predatory maintenance fauna in the present are representative environmental expressions of this regulatory collapse. Individual placements remain subject to their build-facing district and encounter specifications.

## 7. District political economy

Districts should not be aesthetic factions pasted onto levels. Each one is a case study in a distinct coordination failure.

| District | Necessary function | Institutional promise | Economic contradiction | Present-day evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **The facility / Section 3B** | System-wide coordination, simulation, care, assignment, experimental evaluation | See the whole system, reduce destructive conflict, and intervene rationally | The experiment's evaluator becomes the sole source of legibility while losing contact with lived conditions | Clean interiors, scan gates, simulations, wellness systems, rejected tags, absent public body, missing care facade |
| **Plumbing Power Project** | Water, drainage, pressure, flow | Universal circulation through civic engineering | Those who control valves decide whose emergency counts | Grand utility forms, backwash, stuck valves, readable surges |
| **Greenfields Collective** | Housing and domestic provisioning | Remove housing insecurity through shared optimization | Statistical perfection treats difference as error and schedule as community | Identical dwellings, cycling doors, tended beds, little rust, social emptiness |
| **Open Files Initiative** | Records, data, institutional memory | Make information public and decisions accountable | The state preserves legibility by scanning, sorting, and restricting access | Drawer canyons, terminal chains, patrol catwalks, scan arches |
| **The Hypelines** | Iron distribution and logistics | Keep the civilization's “heart” circulating | A shared supply network is captured at its interfaces and conditionally routed body by body | Corporate rebrand over old lettering, conveyors, authorization gates, worn detours |
| **Ancourage** | Heat, industrial safety, power junctions | Make dangerous systems friendly and manageable | Cheerful branding masks risk displaced onto maintenance workers | Burned conduits, mascots, fused faults, bright safety language |
| **Honeycomb Cooperative** | Worker housing, signal and utility support | One worker supports many; federated mutual aid | Inspectable success replaces functioning support, and responsibility is dispersed until nobody can stop the rot | Pristine street face, stripped flank, dummy valves, hollow slabs, bare-wire arcs |
| **Cleanstreets Initiative** | Transit, sanitation, civic space | Safe, efficient circulation for everyone | The commons is optimized against people who need to stop, rest, or travel without recognized authorization | Spikes, standing-only shelters, access gates, memorialized clearances, beaten bypasses |
| **Beacon Hill** | Archives and cultural memory | Preserve the past for the public | Preservation becomes capture: the record survives because people cannot touch it | Rare opening windows, enforcement vestibules, compacted stacks, immaculate gloom |
| **Bulwark Wharf / Filtration** | Barrier maintenance, quarantine, crossing | Protect the inside without stopping necessary exchange | Protection hardens into exclusion while deferred upkeep makes the barrier physically fail | Pore states, blast bulkheads, tag patrols, clamp consoles, weeping breaches |
| **The Aghora** | Informal exchange, repair, comparison, survival routing | Meet needs and desires the allocation system refuses to see | Freedom from official allocation also means no guaranteed floor and no accountable protection | Barter stalls, stolen power, hand prices, lookouts, iron and glucose exchange |
| **NUTECH / the Paranucleus** | Specialized research and technical intervention | Solve failures beyond public capacity | Technical monopoly turns systemic crisis into proprietary opportunity | Corporate base, restricted technical site, biological engulfment of the institution |

### The envy experiment across the districts

The NV Unit premise gives the district differences a common spine:

- **Greenfields** minimizes residential comparison through statistically optimized, visibly similar dwellings. Its failure is not poverty but the inability to accommodate a life that differs from the model.
- **Open Files** manages what residents can compare and know. Public information becomes curated legibility.
- **The Hypelines** hides unequal distribution behind neutral flow language. Nobody is officially privileged; some routes simply recognize more classes, tags, or authorized destinations.
- **Honeycomb** preserves the appearance of equal worker dignity while hidden labor conditions and support failures diverge.
- **Cleanstreets** removes visible stagnation and deprivation from common space, allowing the experiment to report orderly circulation.
- **Beacon Hill** controls comparison across time by determining which past is accessible and when.
- **The facility** evaluates the experiment and therefore has the greatest incentive not to recognize that eliminating envy was insufficient evidence of overall social and material health.
- **The Aghora** is where wanting, bargaining, difference, imitation, resentment, generosity, and taste become visible again.
- **The facility's simulation and supervisors** channel novelty into catalogued, individually approved enhancements rather than changes to the common world.
- **Corporate operators** sell convenience, access mediation, and technical dependency around physical systems without becoming the source of facility credits.

This should prevent the districts from feeling like unrelated political satires. Each one manages a different source or symptom of comparison.

### The city as an iron-regulation apparatus

The ferroptosis premise also gives several districts one material circuit:

- **bonded stores near the facility** sequester iron like ferritin;
- **the Hypelines** transport and meter iron-dependent capacity;
- **Plumbing Power** supplies the flows that keep material moving rather than pooling;
- **Open Files** records claims, inventories, and acceptable concentrations;
- **Bulwark / Filtration** maintains the barriers that keep bound and reactive material in their proper compartments;
- **the Aghora** recovers and converts scrap outside official safety guarantees;
- **domestic siderophores** reclaim iron that escapes the managed circuit;
- **ferroptotic breaches** are the physical consequence of reserve, transport, barrier, and antioxidant regulation failing together.

No district should be able to solve the iron crisis alone. Storage without distribution produces deprivation. Distribution without records produces uncontrolled draw. Records without local observation preserve false inventories. Recovery without husbandry strips living infrastructure. Barriers without maintenance eventually leak.

That interdependence is the economic version of the neurovascular unit itself.

### District writing rule

Every district needs seven answers:

1. What real problem did this institution solve?
2. Who benefited when it worked?
3. How did it participate in satisfying, curating, or privatizing desire?
4. What corrective signal or cross-position relationship did it make illegible?
5. What proxy, chokepoint, or maintenance burden corrupted it?
6. What bounded function continued after feedback disappeared?
7. What physical process still enacts that corruption now?

If a district only has a slogan and a visual style, it is not yet worldbuilding.

## 8. Fauna origins and biological labor

This section formalizes the through-line for the creature redesign.

### 8.1 Do not give every enemy the same origin

The roster supports three origin families:

1. **Domestic service fauna** — imported, bred, or cultivated to perform extraction, filtration, sensing, sanitation, transport, repair, or containment.
2. **Institutional organisms / personnel** — enforcement, immune response, barrier maintenance, signaling, and other classes that belonged to the civilization's own regulatory body.
3. **Collapse-emergent pathologies and opportunists** — tangles, mutations, pre-existing opportunistic colonies, and later adaptations that did not have a legitimate civic job.

The domestication premise should govern the first family strongly. Forcing Naturalizers, Flares, Spikers, or Tanglers to have been pets would weaken both their biology and politics.

The common through-line across all three families is broader than domestication:

> **They are abandoned functions, dysregulated responses, and opportunists flourishing after the relationships that regulated the system failed.**

For the first two families, a function becomes monstrous when it outlives the relationships that told it where, when, for whom, and when to stop. The third family occupies the ecological space those failed relationships create.

### 8.2 The service-fauna lifecycle

**PROPOSAL**

Every domestic service species should have a seven-stage history:

1. **Problem** — a specific bottleneck the civilization could not afford to solve conventionally.
2. **Import** — the biological capability that made the species attractive.
3. **Domestication** — breeding or training that made its behavior predictable and useful.
4. **Dependency** — feed, handler signals, medicine, nesting architecture, culling, or reproductive control required to keep it safe.
5. **Value capture** — the institution or operator that received the benefit.
6. **Abandonment** — the concrete budget, access, or logistics failure that removed its care.
7. **Feral adaptation** — the survival behavior that became its current gameplay threat.

The monster design should visually contain at least three pieces of that history:

- one remnant of the useful anatomy;
- one remnant of domestication or handling;
- one grotesque adaptation that now makes the old function dangerous.

### 8.3 Creature-origin worksheet

Use this before silhouette exploration for **domestic service fauna**. For an institutional actor, replace purchaser and handler with the institution and signal network that regulated it. For a pathology or opportunist, mark civic-job fields not applicable and instead define its native niche or pathological origin, the failed control relationship that released it, and the new niche it occupies.

| Field | Question |
|---|---|
| **Biological inspiration** | What does the real organism or molecule do in its native biological context? |
| **Civic job** | What analogous job did residents hire or breed it to perform? |
| **Purchaser** | Which Project, Collective, Cooperative, Initiative, or company acquired it? |
| **Beneficiary** | Who received the value of its work? |
| **Handler** | Which workers kept it fed, socialized, medicated, limited, and reproductively stable? |
| **Control signal** | What told it where to work, when to stop, and what not to consume? |
| **Hidden cost** | What waste, dependency, injury, or ecological effect was excluded from the success metric? |
| **Abandonment event** | Which supply interruption or institutional decision removed care? |
| **New food source** | What does it consume now that provision has ended? |
| **Feral behavior** | How is its old work transformed into a readable enemy action? |
| **Old-world evidence** | What kennel, feeding station, handler tool, warning sign, invoice, or work song proves the origin in the environment? |
| **Player model** | What prediction should a player be able to make from observing it? |

### 8.4 Siderophores as a first domestic family

**ESTABLISHED:** Sapscraps, Ferrules, Hidras, and Crusts compete for iron and occupy different iron niches. Their modern distribution reveals the condition of infrastructure.

**BIOLOGICAL BOUNDARY:** Real siderophores are small iron-binding molecules produced by microbes; enterobactin is a molecule, not an animal. The fauna are therefore fictional engineered or cultivated organisms that use siderophore-like chemistry and were given collection, return, stop, and husbandry behaviors the molecules themselves do not possess.

**PROPOSAL:** The civilization imported or cultivated siderophore-inspired service fauna as a distributed iron-recovery ecology. In economic terms, they recovered the material reserve that leaked out of official circulation. Different species recovered iron from different environments:

- small mobile scavengers gathered oxidized iron, corrosion products, iron-bearing debris, and spillage;
- breach specialists concentrated iron at damaged interfaces;
- conduit specialists recovered or redistributed iron inside inaccessible infrastructure;
- surface colonies stabilized or stripped embedded iron from walls and pipes.

They were not simply “iron eaters.” A managed population closed an expensive material loop: locating exposed iron, binding labile iron into less-reactive complexes, and returning it to places where handlers could recover or safely sequester it.

Domestication made them dependent on:

- calibrated feed gradients;
- handler pheromones or light signals;
- collection nests where recovered iron could be harvested;
- harvesting of bound iron, inhibitor dosing, controlled iron depletion, or culling to prevent territorial overshoot;
- stable boundaries between species.

When supply coordination failed, they followed the only instruction still encoded in their bodies: find iron. Without collection, calming, and stop signals, infrastructure, residents, and one another became competing reservoirs. The civilization's lost-value recovery system became a force that tears value out of anything still alive.

This origin makes their enemy behaviors consequences of an economy:

- a lunge is a desperate collection behavior;
- camping a breach is monopolizing the richest remaining source;
- stripping a conduit is salvage labor without a stop signal;
- territorial displacement is market competition rendered as ecology.

It also supplies environmental storytelling: abandoned collection baskets, handler gloves, mineral feed bricks, corroded weighing stations, breed marks, failed calming lamps, and “recovery yield” boards that tracked extracted iron but not animal condition.

### 8.5 Enemy design law

An enemy's lore is only useful if it improves play.

For each creature:

- its old function, native niche, or pathological drive must explain its current target selection;
- its survival need must explain where it appears;
- its body must telegraph the action it is about to take;
- its counter must follow from the same biology;
- its environment must let the player infer at least one relationship before combat;
- failure should falsify a reasonable prediction, not reveal an arbitrary exception.

Backstory that does not alter silhouette, behavior, habitat, telegraph, counterplay, or environmental evidence belongs in a secondary codex entry, not the core design.

## 9. Culture and ordinary life

The economy needs evidence of people living inside it, not just institutions failing.

### 9.1 What genuine contentment felt like

**SYNTHESIS**

Life inside the successful simulation should not feel like obvious imprisonment:

- ordinary wants are anticipated or easily formulated;
- the environment is clean, coherent, and responsive;
- residents can pursue art, comfort, identity, pleasure, mastery, and personalized novelty;
- a credited enhancement can be genuinely delightful;
- specialized roles residents embraced provide a comprehensible place in the social whole before those roles harden into assignments;
- painful comparisons arrive softened, contextualized, or already resolved;
- supervisors usually read as helpful stewards rather than arbitrary guards;
- wellness systems offer language for distress and immediate ways to modulate it.

Disconnection appears through absences rather than constant misery:

- conversation circulates around preferences and experiences more often than shared decisions;
- a public problem becomes a feed item, dashboard, ticket, or private accommodation;
- residents know representations of other classes better than people from those classes;
- physical maintenance appears as background continuity rather than someone else's ongoing labor;
- novelty changes the simulation more often than the common world;
- there is little practice in sustaining disagreement long enough to discover something together.

This is why deterioration can advance without a conventional uprising. The population is not waiting to be liberated from an experience it hates. The missing thing is difficult to request because it is the unmediated relation among people, material conditions, and a future they must build together.

### 9.2 Intimacy, names, and mediated love

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

The word **love** has been displaced from ordinary public vocabulary into narrower or commercial registers. Residents more readily speak of lust, hospitality, bonding, and doing acts another person likes. Earlier products such as Simulo-skins and Love Linguistics made relationship into an experience or skill package that could be delivered, learned, and optimized.

This does not mean affection vanished. It means the vocabulary for an unpredictable, unpriced commitment to another person became difficult to preserve. Aster and Peris recover that register privately before they can make it public.

Operational contexts replace names with worker codes. A code makes a person legible as function; using a name becomes intimate because it recognizes someone beyond current use. Tag failure is therefore not merely a database error. It exposes how little institutional personhood remains once the function no longer parses.

### 9.3 State childhood and displaced parenthood

**ESTABLISHED — DIRECTOR CLARIFICATION**

Children are legally property of the state. This is ownership, not merely public schooling, subsidized childcare, or temporary guardianship.

The society justified the arrangement through two linked claims: adults wanted to maximize the fulfillment and potential of their own lives, and many could not carry parental responsibility without producing bad parenting; state ownership was therefore presented as a way to free adults from a responsibility they were handling poorly while maximizing each child's development and potential.

That explanation is the institution's historical rationale, not a neutral finding that all parents were incapable or that ownership follows morally from parenting failure. Whether the arrangement actually improved child welfare, adult fulfillment, or anyone's potential remains **OPEN**. It converted a particular responsibility to a child into an administrative function and treated the child as a state asset.

The exact mechanics remain **OPEN**: where children live; who performs daily care; whether and how biological parents maintain contact; how names, households, and kinship work; when class and subclass are assigned; and what happens when a child or parent resists.

### 9.4 Caste pride and internal policing

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

The caste is beloved because its goods are real. Skill, selective collaboration, mastery, pride, and a recognizable place in the whole can make “what you are built for” feel affirming. The violence appears when the specialty becomes unleaveable and the institution forgets it was ever chosen.

Communities also enforce the envy-free interpretation laterally:

- workers treat systemic questioning as buzzkill behavior that threatens belonging;
- management practices a polite, supposedly respectful class-blindness that prevents material conflict from being named;
- each group knows a real good in its own life and a curated caricature of the other's;
- dissatisfaction remains isolated because neither register can assemble a cross-class account alone.

The right to re-choose is the social form of anamnesis. A specialty can remain meaningful only if a person can become otherwise.

### 9.5 What people valued

**PROPOSAL**

- competence without spectacle;
- keeping a route, pump, household, or organism alive through patient attention;
- mutual reliability between specialized workers;
- the beauty of infrastructure that visibly served life;
- neighborhood knowledge that corrected central abstractions;
- the right to pause without having to justify productivity.

These values make care the credible counterforce to decay. They also explain why characters retain tenderness toward broken systems instead of treating the whole past as a joke.

### 9.6 What status looked like

**SYNTHESIS**

Inside curated perception, the NV Unit made status less conspicuous and harder to name. Physical districts could still differ sharply in housing, rest, infrastructure access, environmental safety, and precarity. Objective advantage appeared through:

- proximity to the central facility;
- a tag that remained valid across districts;
- schedule flexibility;
- access to uncurated information;
- the ability to keep dependents together during reassignment;
- clean air, safe rest, and reliable utilities;
- insulation from inspection and enforcement;
- permission to treat metrics as advisory rather than binding.

The experiment could therefore display relatively uniform visible or simulated consumption while producing profound positional inequality in the physical world. A better drink machine is a legible reward inside Aster's own experience; whether other residents can see one another's enhancements remains open. The more consequential privilege is never needing to wonder whether the gate will recognize you.

### 9.7 Speech and perception registers

**PROPOSAL**

- **Official speech** turns moral decisions into neutral flow management.
- **Corporate speech** turns restriction into convenience and optimization.
- **Collective speech** turns standardization into solidarity.
- **Preservation speech** turns closure into stewardship.
- **Worker speech** names the physical truth: dead line, hungry wall, false valve, flesh toll.
- **Aghora speech** prices what official language refuses to admit has value.

Formal names belong on signs. Vernacular names belong in mouths.

The protagonist pair also divides the world's dominant epistemic registers. Aster reads floor prices, dashboards, patterns, and institutionally produced data; Peris reads felt experience, bodies, relationships, and flora response. Neither register is sufficient alone, and their ability to translate rather than erase one another is part of the cure.

Tag Day, wellness referral, continuity placement, supportive separation, sanction, and reassignment turn coercive decisions into neutral care or flow language. The euphemism should never be merely sinister decoration: it is the vocabulary through which sincere workers remain able to perform the act.

### 9.8 Care and coercion

**SYNTHESIS + PROPOSAL**

Care should not be portrayed as merely propaganda. That would undermine the game's central value.

Instead:

- people perform real care inside harmful structures;
- institutions use the existence of carers to legitimize decisions carers did not control;
- metrics reward care's visible outputs while starving its time and relationships;
- abandoned handlers may be morally compromised and still have loved the animals they tended;
- the humane choice is often maintenance without ownership: tending a being rather than restoring it to service.

## 10. Worldbuilding guardrails

### 10.1 Avoid ideological caricature

Do not write:

- “envy is simply a moral defect the experiment should have cured”;
- “envy is secretly good and suffering must be restored”;
- “contented people cannot create”;
- “competition or inequality is necessary for progress”;
- “equal provision caused the collapse”;
- “the residents' happiness was fake”;
- “supervisors deny enhancements because they are personally malicious”;
- “the collective failed because everyone was lazy”;
- “the market would have fixed it”;
- “the Aghora proves private trade is freedom”;
- “the corporation caused every failure”;
- “the central facility secretly planned the collapse”;
- “all workers were innocent and all administrators malicious”;
- “facility credits purchase physical necessities”;
- “managerial physical-world money and facility credits are the same currency”;
- “iron and simulation credits are two names for the same currency”;
- “more circulation is always good” or “all storage is hoarding”;
- “FELINE is proven medical canon.”

Prefer mechanisms:

- a private enhancement resolved a want before it became a shared claim;
- supervisor review translated an unusual aspiration into the nearest approved category;
- opposite-direction class narratives prevented two accurate partial views from becoming one systemic account;
- low complaint rates concealed the loss of public correction;
- an inspection metric changed construction behavior;
- a tag or route authorization diverted traffic into an unsafe path;
- a maintenance budget protected the visible facade;
- a tag mismatch removed a person's access;
- a warehouse met its stock target while its output interface failed;
- vascular supply remained adequate while local processing and distribution starved a district;
- a handler lost feed authorization and released a population.

### 10.2 Preserve mixed motives

Every major institution should contain:

- a real need;
- a sincere constituency;
- a material achievement;
- a blind spot;
- an incentive to preserve itself;
- a group made illegible by its success metric.

### 10.3 Let the environment make the argument

**ESTABLISHED — GDD**

The thesis is never directly announced in-game. No terminal, antagonist, supervisor, or ending speech should deliver this document's complete diagnosis. Characters hold partial views; the player discovers the chain through mechanics, environments, relationships, and differences among endings.

The strongest political storytelling is causal and playable:

- the legal toll gate is short but costly;
- the worn path is free but exposed;
- the inspected floor fails under real load;
- the archive is preserved but inaccessible;
- the housing is maintained but cannot accommodate a life off schedule;
- the domestic animal still approaches the obsolete feeding lamp before attacking.

Use terminals and dialogue to deepen a mechanism the player has seen, not to substitute for one.

### 10.4 Keep the two readings unresolved

Architecture and enemies should continue to read simultaneously as:

- civic system and living tissue;
- political institution and biological regulation;
- neglected infrastructure and sick body.

Do not announce that one is “the real metaphor.” The friction between both readings is the world.

## 11. Decisions to lock next

These questions materially affect districts, characters, creature origins, and future dialogue.

1. **What did the founders think envy was?** Its status as suffering and comparative signal is now locked; the remaining question is whether the founders blamed material insecurity, visibility, personality, mediated comparison, or several causes.
2. **What counted as experimental success?** Self-report, conflict rates, enhancement satisfaction, wellness scores, social-network stability, or institutional coherence?
3. **Did residents know they were subjects?** Was participation voluntary, inherited by later generations, contractually bounded, or impossible to leave?
4. **Who ran the experiment and appointed supervisors?** An external research body, the central facility, an elected civic structure, NUTECH, or a changing coalition?
5. **Can non-managerial classes access simulation despite rejecting it?** Managerial use is primary; the militaristic class is taught to avoid Psyknapses and simulation, and maintenance culture dismisses them as out of touch. Actual access, exceptions, frequency, purpose, and restrictions remain open, as does the distinction from premium simulo-care.
6. **How do facility credits work inside the simulation?** Earning, transfer, pooling, expiry, visibility, pricing, and inheritance remain open.
7. **How does supervisor approval work?** Criteria, discretion, audit, appeal, automation, typical approval rate, and whether residents can request something outside the catalog remain open.
8. **Which guarantees extend beyond vascular provision?** Universal access to the natural resources required for survival is now locked as the intended function of common circulation. The exact status of housing, care, transit, information, and discretionary physical goods remains open. The detailed topology also remains open: supply may enter through a distributed vascular network or through major inlets before branching outward, but the central facility is not itself the material source.
9. **What vocabulary replaces legacy “credit toll” language?** Route authorization, permit, pass, allocation token, tag clearance, or another unit?
10. **Does iron ever function as literal tender?** Its inelastic material necessity is established; the endogenous-demurrage model is governing synthesis; formal pricing, settlement, barter, and ownership rules remain open.
11. **How literally does iron access determine individual survival?** Does a failed route immediately harm a resident, primarily degrade shared infrastructure, or operate on different timescales?
12. **What limits usable iron?** Binding agents, antioxidant capacity, transport bandwidth, biological compatibility, local processing, handler labor, or all of them?
13. **What does a tag encode?** Identity only, or also job, residence, health, household, experimental cohort, and clearance?
14. **How are the three classes and their subclasses assigned and escaped?** The split, each class's possession of its own justification, the stated non-managerial anti-simulation narratives, and state ownership of children are locked. The relationship between biological parentage and state class assignment, exact subclass placement, movement between classes, and the right to re-choose remain open.
15. **What can reassignment legally change?** Work, housing, family unit, care, district, identity status, or study cohort?
16. **Who owns and governs physical infrastructure?** Public body, district institution, cooperative membership, concession operator, or no meaningful owner?
17. **Who cultivated service fauna?** A civic research body, NUTECH, district projects, or multiple competing breeders?
18. **Why did husbandry fail first?** Feed shortage, handler reassignment, route denial, quarantine, automation, or deliberate release?
19. **What precipitated retrenchment?** A slow iron crisis, disease cascade, barrier failure, political conflict, external isolation, or several mutually reinforcing shocks?
20. **What lies beyond the vascular and mapped world?** “External supply” and “imported” species require an origin even if the player never visits it.
21. **How visible was dissent before collapse?** Was the Aghora tolerated, periodically cleared, secretly useful to officials, or openly oppositional?
22. **What did ordinary people call the central system?** Was “NV Unit” everyday language, an official study name, or a label used mostly by administrators?
23. **How does state ownership of children work in ordinary life?** Residence, carers, parental contact, naming, education, class assignment, resistance, and the point at which state ownership ends remain open.
24. **Where are the physical supply interfaces?** The branching chain is locked, but the number and placement of vascular inlets, processing sites, bonded stores, district depots, and clearance outlets remain open.
25. **Who can reprioritize common supply?** Ordinary allocation authority, emergency release, local override, appeal, mutual aid, and the boundary between managerial coordination and maintenance judgment remain open.

### Recommended first locks

Lock questions 4–9 before revising simulation dialogue or physical access signage. Lock questions 10–12 and 16 before designing shops, tolls, or iron transactions. Lock questions 17–20 before finalizing the fauna roster. The rest can remain productively ambiguous longer.

## 12. Immediate use in creature development

Before generating another enemy image:

1. choose its origin family;
2. complete the relevant domestic, institutional, or opportunist branch of the creature-origin worksheet;
3. define one readable gameplay prediction;
4. identify its purchaser and handler, its regulating institution and signals, or the failed relationship that opened its niche;
5. identify the anatomy that performed the old job or now expresses its native/pathological drive;
6. exaggerate that anatomy around the current attack;
7. include only enough history—domestication residue, institutional marking, or pathological context—to read at gameplay distance.

For fodder enemies, the finished concept should fit one relevant sentence:

> “It used to **do X** for **this institution**; now it **does Y** to survive, so the player can predict **Z**.”

> “It normally responds to **X** under **this regulating signal**; now that signal is gone, it **does Y**, so the player can predict **Z**.”

> “It exploited **this niche** before collapse; failed regulation expanded that niche, so it now **does Y** and the player can predict **Z**.”

If that sentence is weak, more visual detail will not rescue the design.

### 13.1 Research anchors and limits

Useful primary anchors for the biological inspiration include oligodendrocyte secretion of ferritin heavy chain as neuronal protection ([Mukherjee et al., 2020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32531201/)); astrocytic ferroportin in iron delivery during remyelination ([Schulz et al., 2012](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22492039/)); and the counterintuitive clinical result that deferiprone reduced measured brain iron while worsening or failing outcomes in separate Parkinson's and small Alzheimer's trials ([Devos et al., 2022](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36449420/); [Ayton et al., 2024](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamaneurology/fullarticle/2825846)).

Those findings support the design question “what if quantity is a poor proxy for usable distribution?” They do not validate FELINE as a universal causal model. The game should borrow the research's systems questions rather than its confidence level.

## 14. Creative provenance and future acknowledgments

This ledger preserves the human intellectual and artistic lineage of the project so it can later inform end credits, acknowledgments, a bibliography, or an annotated design history. It should distinguish:

- a work that directly inspired a scene or idea;
- the director's personal takeaway from that work;
- the game's transformation of the takeaway;
- a later synthesis introduced while documentation was being consolidated.

An inspiration entry does not imply endorsement of an author's full body of work, scholarly fidelity, or direct adaptation. Conversely, the use of drafting and consolidation tools should not erase the director's original chain of reading, research, association, and invention.
