# Sapscraps Idle Turnaround 01

Generated as four separate images with the built-in image generator. The
bottom-right creature from `sapscraps-short-arm-mouth-exploration-01.png` was
used as the initial design reference. Each subsequent view also used the
completed preceding views to preserve identity.

## Locked design

- Compact, squat, subtly lopsided body resembling a malformed jaw trap.
- Enormous forward-facing central ring mouth with a deep black interior and
  one sparse row of large, irregular, chipped triangular teeth.
- Exactly three extremely short, thick arms: two lower front-side arms planted
  on the ground and one rear-upper arm folded over the crown.
- Each arm ends in one oversized dirty-ivory hook claw: three arms and three
  claws total.
- No separate legs, feet, tail, head, eyes, tongue, lips, horns, or additional
  anatomy.
- Simple common-enemy construction using 5–8 broad low-poly masses.
- Dry bruised-plum and dirty red-violet surfaces, pale teeth and claws,
  restrained rust abrasion, and faded ochre husbandry markings.
- Low, heavy, tense neutral idle pose on a warm off-white paper background.

## Three-quarter prompt

Create one standalone three-quarter front view at creature eye level with a
mild orthographic feel. Show enough of one side and a sliver of the back to
establish volume while keeping the mouth dominant. Both lower arms bear the
weight; the third arm curls compactly over the rear crown.

## Front prompt

Match the completed three-quarter creature exactly. Create a strict,
straight-on frontal model-reference view with the camera centered on the
mouth. The mouth appears nearly circular, both lower arms remain legible, and
the third rear arm curls into view above the crown. Preserve organic
asymmetry rather than mirroring it perfectly.

## Side prompt

Match the completed three-quarter and front references exactly. Create a true
90-degree orthographic left-side profile with the creature facing screen
right. The front mouth becomes a deep foreshortened oval at the right edge;
the rounded blind back occupies the left. Show one near lower arm, part of the
far lower arm, and the folded crown arm. The back is solid, with no second
mouth or through-hole.

## Back prompt

Match all preceding references exactly. Create a true 180-degree orthographic
rear view. The mouth points directly away and is not visible. Show a solid
irregular blind plated back, both lower hook arms, and the folded third hook
centered high on the crown. Continue the rust wear and faded husbandry
markings logically around the rear.

## Shared exclusions

No redesign, long limbs, fourth arm, missing third arm, separate feet, extra
claws, tail, horns, eyes, secondary mouth, through-hole, cute expression,
gore, props, environment, multiple creatures, labels, captions, or inset
views.
