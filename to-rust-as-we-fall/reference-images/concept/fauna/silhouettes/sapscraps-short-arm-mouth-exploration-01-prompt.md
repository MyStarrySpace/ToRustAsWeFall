# Sapscraps Short-Arm Mouth Exploration 01

## Use

Early shape exploration for a common fodder enemy in a voxel-painterly dark
science-fiction game.

## Primary request

Start the present-day Sapscrap design completely over. Explore six low-detail
variations built around a grotesque central ring-mouth with sharp teeth and
exactly three very short, stubby malformed arms ending in claws.

These are feral descendants of a more elegant domesticated iron-reclamation
organism. Generations of neglect compressed and degraded its useful anatomy
into an ugly, hungry scavenger.

## Composition

A clean 2x3 exploration sheet on warm off-white paper. Show six distinct
full-body variants at the same scale in clear three-quarter view. Use one
creature per cell with no labels, text, borders, diagrams, action sequence, or
environment.

## Shared anatomy

- A compact squat body dominated by one large open central ring-mouth.
- A deep circular or irregular triangular-round mouth opening, dark inside,
  surrounded by a simple ring of sharp inward-facing teeth.
- Exactly three arms in approximate C3 radial placement.
- All arms are extremely short, stubby, thick, crooked, and underdeveloped.
  Root-to-claw length is no more than roughly half the mouth diameter.
- Each arm ends in a compact two-pronged claw or paired hooked talons,
  suggesting the ancestral six iron-binding donors.
- No separate legs. It braces, drags, hops, or knuckle-walks on the same three
  arms.
- No head, eyes, nose, conventional torso, or separate face. The mouth is the
  entire facial and body read.

Explore a flattened puck, swollen knuckle, lopsided three-lobed lump, hunched
seed, low triangular crawler, and compact jaw-trap. Vary mouth shape, arm
placement, tooth rhythm, and mass while keeping three short arms of the same
type. Allow subtle asymmetry, fused joints, uneven claws, missing chips, and
off-center weight.

## Role and style

This is a constantly encountered workhorse swarm enemy. It must read instantly
at distance and remain cheap to model and animate. Use only 5–8 major masses,
broad planes, bold negative spaces, and no fine surface clutter.

Mood: hostile, repulsive, starved, scrappy, and slightly pathetic, but not
comedic. Use chunky low-poly or voxel-painterly forms, graphic silhouettes,
matte surfaces, and restrained painterly shading.

Palette: bruised plum, dirty red-violet, black mouth interior, pale chipped
teeth and claws, sparse rust stains, and one or two faint traces of ochre
domestication pigment.

The neutral pose should imply that it can tuck its arms back, compress like a
knuckle, and throw the mouth forward in a short whole-body lunge. The claws
seize and hold the target after impact.

Avoid long limbs, tails, elegant triskelions, transport slings, webbing,
industrial fittings, perfect clean donuts, cute cereal mascots, smiling mouths,
comedy, eyes, tongues, lips, human teeth, finger-hands, conventional
quadrupeds, spider/crab/octopus anatomy, extra limbs, separate legs, excessive
teeth detail, horns, spikes, gore, ornate rendering, labels, and captions.
