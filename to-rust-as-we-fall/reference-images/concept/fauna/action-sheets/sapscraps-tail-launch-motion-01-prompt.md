# Sapscraps Tail-Launch Motion 01

Create a stylized alien fodder-enemy animation concept sheet on warm
off-white paper. One clean 2x2 grid, four sequential poses of the same
creature, consistent three-quarter side view and scale, reading
left-to-right then top-to-bottom. No labels, text, border, or environment.

## Creature design

A simple compact faceted seed/bud-shaped central hub in deep plum and
red-violet, with a few sparing hot-magenta stress seams. It has exactly
three appendages total: two short claw-like forearms and one much larger
load-bearing tail-arm emerging from the rear underside. Every appendage
terminates in a simple fork with exactly two blunt matte bone-white oval
donor pads, six pads total. The rear tail is literally the third chelating
arm, not an extra leg: it supports the body at idle and swings forward to
join the other two during capture. No head, face, eyes, mouth, conventional
legs, or separate feet.

## Poses

1. **Idle / balanced:** The entire body balances on the single tail-arm.
   The tail curves down beneath the hub like a thick muscular kangaroo tail
   or spring stem; its paired white donor pads are planted on the ground and
   visibly carry the creature's weight. The hub perches above it. Both short
   forearms are swept distinctly behind the hub, tucked backward like folded
   wings, so the front silhouette is closed and relaxed. Exaggerate the
   top-heavy balance and make the tail the obvious support.
2. **Wind-up / compression:** The tail-arm compresses into a tight S-curve
   beneath the creature like a loaded spring, paired pads braced against the
   ground. The hub rocks backward and crouches low. The two forearms begin
   swinging from behind the hub around its sides toward the attack direction.
   Magenta seams glow slightly brighter along the compressed tail. Make stored
   force unmistakable.
3. **Launch / attack:** The tail-arm has explosively straightened against the
   ground, launching the hub horizontally forward through the air. The two
   forearms are now fully thrown forward, reaching ahead of the hub as an open
   two-sided clamp around an empty central cavity. The long straightened tail
   trails far behind for this instant, clearly showing that it supplied
   propulsion. Strong readable forward action and a few restrained ground
   flecks, no arrows.
4. **Capture / three-arm cage:** At contact in midair, the tail-arm has whipped
   forward underneath the hub and joins the two forearms. All three forked tips
   converge symmetrically around one small rust-red iron sphere at the central
   cavity; all six bone-white donor pads touch or nearly touch the sphere
   simultaneously. The final silhouette reads as a compact three-way molecular
   clamp, not a bite or spear.

## Style and constraints

Production-friendly game concept art; charming but threatening fodder enemy;
bold exaggerated proportions; only 5–8 large faceted masses; chunky sculptural
planes; clean crisp silhouettes; alien mineral-organic biology; minimal
texture; soft grounding shadows only in grounded poses. Dark plum body,
red-violet planes, limited hot-magenta seams, and matte bone-white pads.

Make the causal motion instantly legible: tail compresses, tail launches,
forearms swing forward, tail joins capture.

Strictly avoid any fourth limb, extra legs, extra claws, missing appendages,
changing anatomy between panels, a tail without its two donor pads, one-arm
spear attacks, humanoid or crab anatomy, tentacle clutter, intricate armor,
mouths, teeth, eyes, horns, gold trim, realistic rendering, captions, arrows,
or duplicate creatures within a panel.
