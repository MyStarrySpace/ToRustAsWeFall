# Inflammashunt Puzzle - Shadow Solution

The Aster-Peris-only execution path for the Inflammashunt puzzle. This doc is additive to [Inflammashunt Puzzle - Full Spec](./inflammashunt_puzzle.md) and covers only the shadow solution.

## What The Shadow Solution Preserves

The puzzle's five-step solution sequence is unchanged.

1. Open the drainage valve.
2. Scrape damp char deposit A.
3. Scrape damp char deposit B.
4. Tend the root tendril.
5. Open the device housing.

The eight central-room interactables are unchanged. The wrong-approach lessons are unchanged. The retrieval is unchanged. The shadow solution is the same five-step sequence executed by Aster and Peris alone.

## What The Shadow Solution Changes

The presented solution distributes the five steps across three characters:

- Aster opens the valve and the device housing.
- Myke scrapes both char deposits.
- Peris tends the root tendril.

The shadow solution distributes the same five steps across two characters:

- Aster opens the valve and the device housing.
- Aster or Peris scrapes both char deposits.
- Peris tends the root tendril.

The actuator side is not the bottleneck. Scraping char is a general action available to any character. Myke's contribution to the presented solution is route information, not unique labor.

The shadow solution's bottleneck is information. Without Myke, Route C is not directly accessible. Aster and Peris must reconstruct Route C's information from Routes A and B plus careful observation of the junction room.

## Route C Information

Myke's route gives two pieces of information:

- `C1`: active Chelators on the corridor floor are clustered around char deposits and feeding on burn residue.
- `C2`: dormant Chelators near the device are arranged in a ring, buffering the device rather than besieging it.

Both are reconstructible without entering the crawlspace.

## Reconstructing C1: Active Chelators Feed On Char

Available signals without Myke:

- Aster's pipe diagram shows the drainage valve connected to flushing infrastructure. The corridor has something the system was built to flush.
- Peris's living root-Chelator junction shows dormant Chelators connected to roots, pacified, and not visibly feeding.
- The junction room shows active Chelators clustering near visible char deposits rather than wandering randomly.

Synthesis:

The corridor contains residue that the valve can flush. Active Chelators cluster at that residue. Dormant, root-connected Chelators do not feed like that. Therefore, the active Chelators are feeding on char.

This is the direct information Myke's grate observation would have provided.

## Reconstructing C2: Dormant Chelators Buffer The Device

Available signals without Myke:

- Peris's living junction confirms that root-connected Chelators can be passive participants in the resolution cycle.
- The junction room shows the dormant Chelator cluster arranged geometrically around the device housing.
- Examination shows the dormant cluster is warm, connected, still, and not feeding.

Synthesis:

The dormant Chelators near the device are in the same pacified state Peris saw underground, and their arrangement is a ring around the housing. They are a buffer, not a threat.

This is the direct information Myke's device-gap observation would have provided.

## Why The Shadow Solution Is Harder

The presented solution gives six explicit route observations. The shadow solution gives four and asks the player to infer the missing two.

The shadow player must:

1. Read both Aster interactables, not just the tempting "thermal reset" log.
2. Read both Peris interactables, especially the living root-Chelator junction.
3. Observe central-room enemy behavior and spatial arrangement.
4. Understand that multiple route perspectives overlap and can substitute for each other.

The route is fair because all information exists in the world. It is harder because two pieces are implied instead of spoken.

## What The Shadow Solution Loses

The presented solution lands the contradiction triangle:

- Aster confidently misreads the terminal.
- Peris confidently misreads the old roots.
- Myke confidently misreads the crawler pressure.

Without Myke, the triangle becomes a two-pronged contradiction. This is acceptable. The shadow solution is an expert path and may lose some first-play narrative weight in exchange for mechanical elegance.

The shadow solution also loses Myke's after-retrieval recognition line if he is absent. That line should not be reassigned. It belongs to Myke.

## Walkthrough

1. Enter Inflammashunt without using Myke's crawlspace route.
2. Aster reads the engineering log and pipe diagram.
3. Peris reads the dead root network and living root-Chelator junction.
4. In the junction room, observe active Chelators clustering near char and dormant Chelators ringing the housing.
5. Infer that char is the active fuel source and the dormant ring is a buffer.
6. Open drainage valve.
7. Scrape damp char A.
8. Scrape damp char B.
9. Tend root tendril.
10. Open housing.

## Edge Cases

If the player has Myke but skips his route, the shadow solution still works mechanically. This is a self-imposed challenge and can be left alone.

If the player reaches the puzzle before recruiting Myke, the shadow solution is the only clean route. This is consistent with the principle that Aster and Peris can solve every major puzzle through careful play.

If the player lacks the reconstruction but tries anyway, the brute-force wrong-approach pedagogy from the full spec still teaches the solution over time.
